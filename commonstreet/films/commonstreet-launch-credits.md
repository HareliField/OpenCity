# Commonstreet project launch film

The film combines staged and recorded Commonstreet scenes, a wireframe rendered from the project's Lighthouse Cove model, original motion graphics and music, and synthesized narration. English captions are included in the MP4.

The real-world reference and workshop footage is included in the edited film under the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/).
