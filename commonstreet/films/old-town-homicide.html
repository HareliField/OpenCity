Homicide outside Verve Nail Studio

The finding

Toby Lin saw Opal Finch shoot Jude Wick outside Verve Nail Studio and called 911. Officer Ruth Kim led the five-person patrol team and logged three exhibits, a sample from Wick and two prints from the dropped holdall. At Old Town headquarters, analyst Ada Flynn matched one print to Finch. The match corroborated Lin’s identification. Detective Kai Chen submitted the finding, and Commander Maya Scott authorized a wanted bulletin.
