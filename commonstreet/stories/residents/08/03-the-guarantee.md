## Sonia Arnett

AH-R1071 · Age 35 · Business borrower · Home — the neighborhood above Lighthouse Cove

**Life in the city.** “I still remember Mum standing the cost of my very first market table.” Monday often includes a stop at the corner by Juniper Tailor, where Sonia looks at the things people actually bring in for repair before looking in the display window.

**In their own words.** “I assumed Mum put down savings. I found out when she wouldn't lend me the money for a replacement boiler.”

**What Sonia wants.** To work out whether the business can release the house.

**Family.** [Ida Boyd](../06/10-one-paid-trial.md#ah-r0842) is Sonia’s cousin raised in the same household. Sonia chooses whether to use it. Ida saves a place. The invitation means something because declining it has never ended the friendship between their households. Asked about *One paid trial*, Ida says, “I have done the unglamorous closing shift for eleven months.”

**People in this story.** [Sofia Ayala](#ah-r1072) (mother). Daughter and mother have to discuss different meanings of a generous guarantee. Also in the story is [Grant Penn](#ah-r1074) (sibling).

**Personality.** Sonia is firm about boundaries and unusually forgiving of somebody who admits to crossing one. Demands to act grateful for unwanted help can end a friendship.

**Appearance.** Sonia is easy to recognize by short, uneven waves that refuse to lie flat and heavy brows and an unusually delicate jaw. Usually dressed in a plum-colored cardigan with a little coil of soft measuring tape, Sonia touches a knuckle to the chin when searching for a word when listening.

**Under pressure**

- A borrower learns exactly what the guarantee cost. Sonia puts the actual pledged property and repayment date on paper.
- A customer is short at the till while a queue forms. Sonia asks the cashier quietly which item can be set aside without making the customer explain their finances to the line.
- If a relative arrives with a solution nobody requested, Sonia thanks them for coming and explains the part that still belongs to the household.
