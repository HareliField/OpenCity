## Ines Baldwin

AH-R0871 · Age 17 · Music scholarship pupil · Home — the western residential streets

**Life in the city.** “I still remember a viola lesson squeezed between two school runs.” People around the brick passage off the shop row know Ines by a small Thursday habit. Ines takes the longer way when the smell from the ovens reaches the pavement.

**In their own words.** “I won the tuition and haven't dared tell my little sister that going means leaving her school runs to somebody else.”

**What Ines wants.** To accept tuition only with workable travel and care arrangements.

**Family.** [Charlotte Barker](../02/15-a-sail-cut-small.md#ah-r0297) is Ines’s older relative by marriage. Ines brings something that can still be enjoyed tomorrow. Charlotte checks the date twice. Arrangements do change in their family. Taking a delay seriously without treating it as an insult took both of them years. Asked about *A sail cut small*, Charlotte says, “I took the seam apart strand by strand after my hands stopped tolerating heavy rope.”

**People in this story.** [Benedict Perreault](#ah-r0876) (market manager). The market manager's promised Saturday post gives the pupil one reliable foothold. Also in the story is [Sadie Liu](#ah-r0872) (aunt).

**Personality.** Likes beautiful, unnecessary things and gets cross with people who mistake poverty for a lack of taste. Ines takes longer choosing a cheap birthday card than an expensive coat.

**Appearance.** Ines has heavy brows and an unusually delicate jaw. Look for a blunt cut just below the ears. Outside school, the familiar outfit is a faded indigo overshirt with a small enamel bird on the lapel. In conversation Ines grins on one side while the eyes stay serious.

**Under pressure**

- The school wants a deposit before travel support is settled. Ines gets the school to specify the last date a deposit can be returned.
- Smoke appears under a shop door after closing. Ines gets the people at the next doorway away from the smoke and asks a bystander to make the call.
- When a relative dismisses a little luxury, Ines asks which ordinary pleasure the relative would give up first.

## Sadie Liu

AH-R0872 · Age 45 · Aunt and tailor · Home — the streets inland from Pier Beach

**Life in the city.** “I still remember pricing a used score with the corrections left in.” People around the foot of Harbor Viaduct know Sadie by a small Tuesday habit. Sadie pauses where the machinery overhead makes two people stand close enough to hear each other.

**In their own words.** “I promised to cover books until I saw the price of a single score.”

**What Sadie wants.** To price the scores before promising to cover them.

**Family.** [Laura Osborne](../07/10-an-old-restaurant-debt.md#ah-r0995) is Sadie’s older cousin. Sadie brings a spare shopping bag. Laura chooses whether they walk home or sit a while. That small arrangement gives both a reason to see each other on weeks when asking for company outright feels difficult. Asked about *An old restaurant debt*, Laura says, “I am a cousin who contributed nothing then.”

**People in this story.** [Terry Aranda](#ah-r0879). An aunt pricing sheet music learns about the deposit that sank another scholarship. Also in the story is [Ines Baldwin](#ah-r0871) (niece or nephew).

**Personality.** Sadie can be wonderfully decisive about an emergency and maddeningly undecided about a gift. This is because a person can say precisely what hurts and rarely says exactly what they want.

**Appearance.** Sadie is easy to recognize by a center part and straight chestnut hair and full cheeks and a mouth that pulls slightly to one side. Usually dressed in a rust-colored canvas coat with a folded shopping list secured by a miniature clip, Sadie takes off the watch before a long conversation when listening.

**Under pressure**

- A relative urges the pupil to accept in public. Sadie lets the pupil answer privately before relatives announce an acceptance.
- A colleague says somebody used the wrong name in public. Sadie repeats the correct name quietly, making the correction easy to hear without turning it into a performance.
- If a neighbor offers a costly present, Sadie asks for an afternoon to work out whether accepting it would put someone else in debt.

**People in this story.** [Talia Wong](#ah-r0877). A runner-up's teacher helps the fundraiser keep the winner's choice open.

**Appearance.** Morgan has a dark undercut with a longer wave on top, a flat bridge to the nose and a mobile brow, and a favorite piece of clothing, a long navy raincoat. ## Karim Klein

**Life in the city.** “I still remember taking a night's rest beside my own instrument case.” Tuesday often includes a stop at the window at Night Shift Donuts, where Karim knows when the going-home crowd and the coming-to-work crowd will both be there.

**In their own words.** “I used to sleep in a practice room. I told the pupil because I don't want that passed off as romantic.”

**What Karim wants.** To find accommodation that doesn't repeat the practice-room years.

**Family.** [Byron Sato](../10/09-a-house-divided-kindly.md#ah-r1431) is Karim’s second cousin. An old family mantel clock gained three minutes a day. People who couldn't bear to be late liked it. Everybody else wondered why the household was always the first to arrive. Karim shows up early and finds a small job until things start. Asked about *A house divided kindly*, Byron says, “I spent years keeping my hedge straighter than hers.”

**People in this story.** [Ren Su](#ah-r0878). A former musician who slept at school understands the price of a clean practice shirt.

**Personality.** Karim carries anger into a conversation very slowly. Once it arrives, a quiet statement of facts can be considerably harder to sidestep than a shout.

**Appearance.** Karim has silver temples and a dark fringe, a heart-shaped face and prominent ears, and a favorite piece of clothing, an olive canvas jacket. It usually goes with a pen clipped inside the neckline. When somebody else has the floor, Karim gathers both sleeves neatly before settling in.

**Under pressure**

- A relative urges the pupil to accept in public. Karim lets the pupil answer privately before relatives announce an acceptance.
- A customer is short at the till while a queue forms. Karim offers the shortfall once and accepts the answer, including a refusal.
- If a friend asks for a false alibi for an ordinary missed appointment, Karim offers to accompany them while they tell the truth.

## Benedict Perreault

AH-R0876 · Age 47 · Market manager · Home — the neighborhood above Lighthouse Cove

**What Benedict wants.** To protect a Saturday job for the student.

**Family.** [Pearl Gross](../03/01-spin-cycle-choir.md#ah-r0307) is Benedict’s first cousin once removed. Benedict asks the question the rest of the family avoided. Pearl chooses an ordinary story to tell. They tend to get further with an important subject when it arrives in the middle of talk about dinner or work. **People in this story.** [Ines Baldwin](#ah-r0871) (market worker). The market manager's promised Saturday post gives the pupil one reliable foothold.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a brown corduroy jacket and a small enamel bird on the lapel. Benedict has one cheek that creases before the other, with a swept fringe and a closely clipped nape. Benedict holds eye contact until the other person has finished during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- A relative urges the pupil to accept in public. Benedict lets the pupil answer privately before relatives announce an acceptance.
- A colleague says somebody used the wrong name in public. Benedict asks which name they want to hear next time and uses it in the next ordinary introduction.
- When offered a favor with ambiguous terms, Benedict says what can be returned and asks whether that will do.

## Terry Aranda

AH-R0879 · Age 45 · Former music student and bookbinder · Home — Old Town

**Life in the city.** “I still remember an acceptance letter I never got to use.” On Saturday, near the steps at Pier Beach, Terry watches the working part of the waterfront before turning toward the open water.

**In their own words.** “My own scholarship fell apart over an unpaid deposit. I keep the letter in my work bag for a reason.”

**What Terry wants.** To prevent an unpaid deposit from costing another child the place.

**Family.** [Harry Rhodes](../10/14-the-last-home-visit.md#ah-r1488) is Terry’s older cousin. When an older relative began losing nouns, they drew a map of places where familiar words came back. The map still gets folded correctly every time it comes out. Terry marks the places where keeping quiet worked. Asked about *The last home visit*, Harry says, “I can reach my own medicine from a lower shelf.”

**People in this story.** [Sadie Liu](#ah-r0872). An aunt pricing sheet music learns about the deposit that sank another scholarship.

**Personality.** Terry is fond of ambitious ideas and deeply suspicious of grand openings. The question Terry asks at any launch is who has the keys for cleaning up a month later.

**Appearance.** Terry has black hair twisted into a small knot, a round face with a pronounced chin, and a favorite piece of clothing, a charcoal zip cardigan. It usually goes with one cuff folded back twice. When somebody else has the floor, Terry draws breath as if to speak and lets someone else go first.

**Under pressure**

- The school wants a deposit before travel support is settled. Terry gets the school to specify the last date a deposit can be returned.
- A family supper turns into an argument about old debt. Terry asks each person to say what they believe is actually owed before anybody proposes a payment.
- If offered a ceremonial role, Terry accepts only after finding out whether the person doing the regular work can come too.

## Benedict Lund

AH-R0880 · Age 33 · Music shop driver · Home — Eastside

**Life in the city.** “I still remember the pupil who came back in February to ask for a lift.” On Saturday, near the blocks below Foundry Lofts, Benedict checks the little hand-lettered notices around Harbour Supply.

**Family.** [Ian Palmer](../02/15-a-sail-cut-small.md#ah-r0295) is Benedict’s cousin raised in the same household. Benedict chooses the music. Ian puts the kettle on. The cups come out before either brings up the reason for the visit. They trust a difficult conversation more if it has had an ordinary beginning. Asked about *A sail cut small*, Ian says, “I can make six small sails for beginners. Their parents asked why every panel was a different shade.”

**People in this story.** [Elias Hart](#ah-r0875). A proposed host family's curfew has to work on the far less glamorous February journeys too.

**Personality.** Benedict is firm about boundaries and unusually forgiving of somebody who admits to crossing one. Demands to act grateful for unwanted help can end a friendship.

**Appearance.** Benedict is easy to recognize by auburn hair cut square at the nape and one cheek that creases before the other. Usually dressed in a rust-colored canvas coat with a pair of reading glasses in a cloth sleeve, Benedict raises one eyebrow long before saying yes when listening.

**Under pressure**

- A relative urges the pupil to accept in public. Benedict lets the pupil answer privately before relatives announce an acceptance.
- A street musician can't finish a familiar song. Benedict offers a little space from the crowd and waits for the player to indicate whether company is wanted.
- If a relative arrives with a solution nobody requested, Benedict thanks them for coming and explains the part that still belongs to the household.
