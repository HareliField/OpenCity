# The blue suit

[The people you meet first](README.md) · [City directory](../directory.md)

**Wash House.** A blue suit comes out of a washer at the Wash House streaked white on the morning of a citizenship ceremony. The owner has ninety minutes and exactly one change of clothes.

## Ida Mercer

AH-R0001 · Age 52 · Laundromat operator · Home — the blocks north of the port

**Life in the city.** Before the accident Ida collected fares and knew regulars by the noises they made climbing onto the bus. Running the Wash House lets her keep that skill. A person dropping one sock at a time needs a chair before they need a lecture about the machines. People around Paper & Pine Books know Ida by a small Wednesday habit. Ida starts with the handwritten shelf cards and often leaves with a suggestion for somebody else.

**In their own words.** “I bought this place with the settlement from my bus accident. I can refund a wash. I can't refund this morning. I gave him the good jacket I keep behind the till for meetings with the bank.”

**What Ida wants.** To keep an emergency clothes rail behind the till without turning it into charity theater.

**Family.** [Bianca Ward](../07/03-a-widow-bids-again.md#ah-r0924) is Ida’s second cousin. Ida makes the call. Bianca is free to hang up first. Some of their best calls last less than a minute. A change of tone is sometimes all the news either one needs. Asked about *A widow bids again*, Bianca says, “I sell luxury property. This is the first bidder who asked me the names on the doorbells.”

**People in this story.** [Rafael Nunes](#ah-r0003). The jacket from behind the till is the loan between them that makes the ceremony possible.

**Personality.** Ida is patient with a person learning and fiercely impatient with somebody pretending to know. A half-finished job left where a beginner can see it is an invitation, as far as Ida is concerned.

**Appearance.** Ida is easy to recognize by a soft fringe that falls across one eyebrow and fine brows over wide-set eyes. Usually dressed in a red windbreaker with a tiny red thread tied through the lowest buttonhole, Ida raises one eyebrow long before saying yes when listening.

**Under pressure**

- Rafael asks everyone to stop fussing over the suit. Ida steps away from the hanger and lets Rafael choose what help to keep.
- A power cut leaves two neighbors waiting on the stairs. Ida stays where both neighbors can hear a familiar voice and gets news from the caretaker.
- When someone invents an answer, Ida asks them to try it in front of the person who will have to live with it.

## Lev Bellamy

AH-R0002 · Age 61 · Tailor · Home — the blocks north of the port

**Life in the city.** Lev began by cutting down men's coats for children during a bad winter. He still saves the narrow strips from the inside because they show what the cloth looked like new. He hates it when people ask to be made thinner. Sunday often includes a stop at the corner by Juniper Tailor, where Lev looks at the things people actually bring in for repair before looking in the display window.

**In their own words.** “My father taught me to turn a ruined cuff inside out. The dye reached the lining this time. I asked the man where he wanted the white stripe to sit and waited for him to choose.”

**What Lev wants.** To give Rafael the option of keeping one visible mark from the morning.

**Family.** [Lennon Wang](../10/12-three-minutes-at-the-bridge.md#ah-r1470) is Lev’s younger cousin. Lev leaves the first voice message. Lennon calls back when there is finally a little privacy. In their family a missed ring can mean a bus, a customer, a sleeping child or a bad hour. Neither treats it as an answer in itself. Asked about *Three minutes at the bridge*, Lennon says, “I had hoped the shared rides would turn into a friendship.”

**People in this story.** [Marnie Phelps](#ah-r0004). They spread the ruined sleeve on the maintenance table to see what can still be saved.

**Personality.** Lev loves an audience until a private hurt enters the room. Then the performer disappears and someone very good at getting other people through a door takes over.

**Appearance.** Lev has a small notch in the left eyebrow. Look for a gray streak running back from the forehead. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a red windbreaker with a fabric zip pull cut from a school scarf. In conversation Lev leans a shoulder against the wall during long conversations.

**Under pressure**

- Someone wants to post the ruined cuff online. Lev asks Rafael which version he wants a stranger to see before anyone shares the picture.
- An unfamiliar person asks for directions but cannot read the street signs. Lev finds out what the destination actually looks like to the traveler and gives directions in those terms.
- If a stranger asks to hear a private story, Lev offers one about their own embarrassment instead.

## Rafael Nunes

AH-R0003 · Age 48 · New citizen and lift installer · Home — Old Town

**Life in the city.** Rafael practiced the oath in the shaft between floors because an elevator counterweight makes a forgiving audience. His daughter never heard him make a mistake. That gave her the false impression that the words came easily. People around Studio Boulevard know Rafael by a small Friday habit. Rafael waits by the window where the whole crossing can be seen without leaning into the road.

**In their own words.** “My wife pressed that suit on the kitchen table the night before she died. I went to the ceremony in a borrowed jacket. The spoiled one came home with me on its own hanger.”

**What Rafael wants.** To get the cuff photograph framed for his daughter before she leaves home.

**Family.** [Albert Hsu](../09/13-the-school-photograph-that-travels.md#ah-r1325) is Rafael’s second cousin. Rafael chooses the familiar route. Albert can stop the walk at any corner. Walking side by side makes it easier to disagree without turning each disagreement into a contest. Asked about *The school photograph that travels*, Albert says, “I drove the school route. She wrote my bus number on the photo because she never knew my surname.”

**People in this story.** [Ida Mercer](#ah-r0001). The jacket from behind the till is the loan between them that makes the ceremony possible. Also in the story is [Alma Faye](#ah-r0009) (portraitist).

**Personality.** Rafael enjoys routines and will defend the right of someone else to break one. As a result, Rafael is often everyone's favorite cover for a missed morning.

**Appearance.** Rafael has a crooked front tooth visible when laughing. Look for short copper curls. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is an olive canvas jacket with a pocket notebook shaped by years of sitting on it. In conversation Rafael steadies a cup with both hands while listening. The borrowed jacket is a little broad over Rafael’s shoulders. He keeps the photographs with the bleached cuff showing.

**Under pressure**

- Rafael asks everyone to stop fussing over the suit. Rafael steps away from the hanger and lets Rafael choose what help to keep.
- A traveler asks for the last seat at the bus stop. Rafael asks the person already using it whether they can comfortably stand, rather than presuming who needs it most.
- If a family plan changes at the door, Rafael keeps a place for the late person without making the others postpone their meal.

## Marnie Phelps

AH-R0004 · Age 29 · Wash attendant · Home — the neighborhood above Lighthouse Cove

**Life in the city.** Marnie used to be trusted with a hotel boiler and nothing else. Leaving for a tiny laundry meant a pay cut and a chance to sign a maintenance sheet with her own name. That signature means more to her than the uniform did. People around the foot of Harbor Viaduct know Marnie by a small Thursday habit. Marnie pauses where the machinery overhead makes two people stand close enough to hear each other.

**In their own words.** “I signed the machine off yesterday because the test towel came back clean. I've put my initials beside the failed test this time. I want the next shift to know exactly who to wake.”

**What Marnie wants.** To secure paid time to test machines between overnight and day shifts.

**Family.** [Stevie Rose](../04/07-a-borrowed-name.md#ah-r0520) is Marnie’s older cousin. Marnie brings the paper and a pen. Stevie decides what stays off the paper. They have helped each other make decisions that were far easier to discuss once the figures could be moved around. The decisions themselves still belong to the person asking. Asked about *A borrowed name*, Stevie says, “I dislike the book and adore the man. I'm organizing a dinner where nobody has to choose.”

**People in this story.** [Lev Bellamy](#ah-r0002). They spread the ruined sleeve on the maintenance table to see what can still be saved.

**Personality.** Marnie is cheerful on a bad morning and unexpectedly melancholy after a success. Finishing a hard thing means losing the daily company of the people who worked on it.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a black overshirt with pearl buttons and a pair of reading glasses in a cloth sleeve. Marnie has heavy brows and an unusually delicate jaw, with short, uneven waves that refuse to lie flat. Marnie listens with the head tilted a little to the left during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- Someone wants to post the ruined cuff online. Marnie asks Rafael which version he wants a stranger to see before anyone shares the picture.
- An unfamiliar person asks for directions but cannot read the street signs. Marnie walks with them as far as the first landmark and points to the next one from there.
- When everyone else wants to celebrate immediately, Marnie helps set the table, then takes a short turn outside alone.

## Salma Ilyas

AH-R0005 · Age 37 · Courthouse interpreter · Home — the western blocks near Ferry Street Rooms

**Life in the city.** As a schoolchild Salma translated every official letter for her family except the one about her own attendance. She makes families tell her which person has asked for the translation before agreeing to speak. On Friday, near the public end of the South Shore boardwalk, Salma checks the shade on the benches before deciding how long to stay.

**In their own words.** “At my own ceremony nobody could pronounce my mother's name. I wrote the pronunciation on his appointment slip and called ahead about the delay while he changed.”

**What Salma wants.** To make the courthouse call a person's chosen name correctly on the first try.

**Family.** [Martin Henry](../08/03-the-guarantee.md#ah-r1077) is Salma’s second cousin. Salma calls about good news first. Martin is allowed to ask about the bad news afterward. Neither wants every family conversation to begin with a bill, a diagnosis or an apology. Asked about *The guarantee*, Martin says, “My parents owned nothing they could pledge.”

**People in this story.** [Dora Beck](#ah-r0008). One is waiting for the courthouse to pick up while the other labels the failed valve.

**Personality.** Salma finds names easy and faces hard on a crowded street. People who assume they have been snubbed usually get a warmer greeting once they speak.

**Appearance.** Salma has fine brows over wide-set eyes. Look for a cropped cut that shows the shape of the ears. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a pale blue work shirt with a printed handkerchief folded into the pocket. In conversation Salma listens with the head tilted a little to the left.

**Under pressure**

- Rafael asks everyone to stop fussing over the suit. Salma steps away from the hanger and lets Rafael choose what help to keep.
- A traveler asks for the last seat at the bus stop. Salma gives up their own space if possible and asks the passengers who arrived first to choose what happens next.
- When somebody waves from across traffic, Salma waits at the corner to hear their voice and give them a proper answer.

## Warren Lyle

AH-R0006 · Age 44 · Insurance adjuster · Home — the neighborhood around Seabright Court

**Life in the city.** Warren took an office job after hot linen burned his forearm. He is still quickest to spot a bad laundry claim by looking at the worker's hands. A neat report with no trace of the people who did the work makes him uneasy. People around the crossing outside Verve Nail Studio know Warren by a small Monday habit. Warren notices whose chair is empty and who has stayed talking after an appointment.

**In their own words.** “I used to work on a hotel laundry line. I found three identical claims under three different machine numbers. I want the service company to come and point to each machine in person.”

**What Warren wants.** To settle the failed-valve claim before the Wash House loses its hotel work.

**Family.** [Nadia Holland](../03/10-the-broken-till.md#ah-r0396) is Warren’s younger cousin. Warren remembers the birthdays. Nadia asks what the birthday guest would like this year. A present that was perfect at sixteen may be completely wrong at forty. They take the trouble of finding out. Asked about *The broken till*, Nadia says, “I wrote everyone's sandwiches in pictures. The cook hung the sheet beside the menu.”

**People in this story.** [Dee Varma](#ah-r0010). Their next conversation will decide what evidence the hotel needs before sending its linen here.

**Personality.** Is a careful borrower and a reckless lender. Things leave this person's house with elaborate instructions and very little expectation of ever coming back.

**Appearance.** Warren has two dimples that appear only with a full smile. Look for short sandy hair and a marked widow's peak. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a charcoal zip cardigan with a pen clipped inside the neckline. In conversation Warren gives a little nod before asking a hard question.

**Under pressure**

- Someone wants to post the ruined cuff online. Warren asks Rafael which version he wants a stranger to see before anyone shares the picture.
- Somebody finds a lost wallet outside the bank. Warren takes it to a staffed counter with a second person present and leaves contact details for the owner.
- If a stranger returns something damaged, Warren listens first to the account of how it was actually used.

## Kit Salcedo

AH-R0007 · Age 34 · Shoe shiner · Home — Coast Ridge

**Life in the city.** Kit rented the shoeshine stool after losing a restaurant job. He makes enough on hearing days to skip the humiliating half-shift his old manager still offers him. People tipping too generously put that pride in a difficult place. On Sunday, near Studio Boulevard, Kit waits by the window where the whole crossing can be seen without leaning into the road.

**In their own words.** “I gave the borrowed shoes a polish on the bank steps. He kept offering me a note. I finally took it because he was starting to feel like everybody's charity case.”

**What Kit wants.** To earn enough on ordinary paid shines to keep the stool on the bank steps.

**Family.** [Shay Lloyd](../02/04-the-second-dog.md#ah-r0184) is Kit’s older cousin. A parent had a second job that nobody mentioned at school. Years later the family discovered two sets of friends who thought they knew entirely different people. Kit can usually spot the person covering a double shift. Asked about *The second dog*, Shay says, “I fostered both litters. Their ears fold the same way until somebody says dinner.”

**People in this story.** [Alma Faye](#ah-r0009). One finishes the borrowed shoes while the other frames the cuff that won't match them.

**Personality.** Kit is kind in very practical ways and prickly about being thanked in public. Kit loves being recognized by the people who notice what happens after the guests have left.

**Appearance.** Kit is easy to recognize by a center part and straight chestnut hair and a flat bridge to the nose and a mobile brow. Usually dressed in a soft blue checked shirt with a replacement button a shade darker than the others, Kit gathers both sleeves neatly before settling in when listening.

**Under pressure**

- Rafael asks everyone to stop fussing over the suit. Kit steps away from the hanger and lets Rafael choose what help to keep.
- A frightened child is waiting outside a shuttered shop. Kit lets the child choose whether to stand by the door or sit beside the window while another adult gets help.
- If somebody calls for applause, Kit gives the credit to a specific helper and slips out before the photograph.

## Dora Beck

AH-R0008 · Age 46 · Mechanic · Home — the neighborhood around Seabright Court

**Life in the city.** Dora spent her first decade fixing appliances others deemed uneconomic. She can price every part on sight and badly underprices her own time. Her brother has stopped lending her the van unless she puts labor on the bill. On Tuesday, near Maré Coffee, Dora likes the table where people coming off shift stop checking the time.

**In their own words.** “The rinse valve came from a batch I signed for with my brother's pencil. I paid for a new one out of the van money and kept the old part for the supplier to examine.”

**What Dora wants.** To get the supplier to inspect the old valve with the workers present.

**Family.** [Sonia Lee](../02/03-the-sandwich-board.md#ah-r0180) is Dora’s cousin. Dora puts the kettle on. Sonia chooses the music. The cups come out before either brings up the reason for the visit. They trust a difficult conversation more if it has had an ordinary beginning. Asked about *The sandwich board*, Sonia says, “I'm the one who wiped off the prices. I wanted people to ask what the cheap lunch actually bought.”

**People in this story.** [Salma Ilyas](#ah-r0005). One is waiting for the courthouse to pick up while the other labels the failed valve.

**Personality.** Likes beautiful, unnecessary things and gets cross with people who mistake poverty for a lack of taste. Dora takes longer choosing a cheap birthday card than an expensive coat.

**Appearance.** Dora has brown hair pinned away from the temples, a heart-shaped face and prominent ears, and a favorite piece of clothing, a brown corduroy jacket. It usually goes with a little coil of soft measuring tape. When somebody else has the floor, Dora squares up pieces of paper while thinking.

**Under pressure**

- Someone wants to post the ruined cuff online. Dora asks Rafael which version he wants a stranger to see before anyone shares the picture.
- A neighbor offers to pay for a favor that was meant kindly. Dora asks for help with a task the neighbor does well, so both people get to contribute something.
- When a relative dismisses a little luxury, Dora asks which ordinary pleasure the relative would give up first.

## Alma Faye

AH-R0009 · Age 33 · Portrait photographer · Home — Coast Ridge

**Life in the city.** Alma learned portraits taking passport pictures behind a copy counter. She likes the half-second after a person stops smiling on instruction. Brides pay her bills, but that half-second is what makes her keep the camera. Wednesday often includes a stop at the bank steps, where Alma takes a few minutes to read the public notices, especially the ones written in a hurry.

**In their own words.** “In the photograph you can see one white cuff. His daughter asked me to take it out. He asked me to leave it in. I made him the first print and put the negative away.”

**What Alma wants.** To give Rafael final say over which portrait leaves the studio.

**Family.** [Jody Graves](../02/10-the-bitter-orange.md#ah-r0241) is Alma’s cousin. Alma chooses an ordinary story to tell. Jody asks the question the rest of the family avoided. They tend to get further with an important subject when it arrives in the middle of talk about dinner or work. Asked about *The bitter orange*, Jody says, “I remember mustard because she'd send it back if we skimped.”

**People in this story.** [Kit Salcedo](#ah-r0007). One finishes the borrowed shoes while the other frames the cuff that won't match them. Also in the story is [Rafael Nunes](#ah-r0003) (client).

**Personality.** Alma can be wonderfully decisive about an emergency and maddeningly undecided about a gift. This is because a person can say precisely what hurts and rarely says exactly what they want.

**Appearance.** Alma has thick, tightly coiled hair, heavy brows and an unusually delicate jaw, and a favorite piece of clothing, an old plum windbreaker. It usually goes with a comb in a sleeve sewn into the lining. When somebody else has the floor, Alma starts to lift a hand, then waits to be invited to speak.

**Under pressure**

- Rafael asks everyone to stop fussing over the suit. Alma steps away from the hanger and lets Rafael choose what help to keep.
- Smoke appears under a shop door after closing. Alma calls for emergency assistance from outside and waits to give the street number.
- If a neighbor offers a costly present, Alma asks for an afternoon to work out whether accepting it would put someone else in debt.

## Dee Varma

AH-R0010 · Age 51 · Hotel linen buyer · Home — the neighborhood above Lighthouse Cove

**Life in the city.** Dee once walked into the staff changing room in a skirt stiff with laundry chemicals. The housekeepers laughed and washed it for her. She has asked them to judge every linen sample since. On Wednesday, near the corner by Juniper Tailor, Dee looks at the things people actually bring in for repair before looking in the display window.

**In their own words.** “My hotel contract would keep the place open through winter. My staff wash their own clothes here too. I offered a week of trial loads with one of our housekeepers choosing the test pieces.”

**What Dee wants.** To sign a contract that lets the housekeepers reject a bad test load.

**Family.** [Jade Hunter](../08/05-the-boy-on-the-corner.md#ah-r1097) is Dee’s half-sibling. Dee checks the date twice. Jade brings something that can still be enjoyed tomorrow. Arrangements do change in their family. Taking a delay seriously without treating it as an insult took both of them years. Asked about *The boy on the corner*, Jade says, “I want to be their foster aunt. My flat is smaller than the official assessor expected and larger than the boys remember.”

**People in this story.** [Warren Lyle](#ah-r0006). Their next conversation will decide what evidence the hotel needs before sending its linen here.

**Personality.** Dee can be lavish with time and quite careful with cash. A free afternoon is sometimes the biggest gift Dee can afford to give, and family knows to take it seriously.

**Appearance.** Dee is easy to recognize by a loose dark braid and full cheeks and a mouth that pulls slightly to one side. Usually dressed in a rust-colored canvas coat with a narrow brass watch set a little fast, Dee counts on the side of one finger where hardly anybody notices when listening.

**Under pressure**

- Someone wants to post the ruined cuff online. Dee asks Rafael which version he wants a stranger to see before anyone shares the picture.
- A witness is embarrassed about remembering only a little. Dee tells them which ordinary detail proved helpful on a previous occasion, then listens without suggesting an answer.
- If invited to an expensive outing, Dee suggests meeting afterward and explains why without asking the others to abandon their plans.
