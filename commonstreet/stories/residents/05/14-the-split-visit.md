## Nathan Neal

AH-R0731 · Age 39 · Home-care worker · Home — the western residential streets

**Life in the city.** “I still remember sitting beside a client nobody had time to hear.” On Tuesday, near Harbour Bakery, Nathan buys one familiar thing and asks who chose the unfamiliar thing in the window.

**In their own words.** “I stayed for breakfast because the woman always eats faster when she thinks we're in a hurry.”

**What Nathan wants.** To let the client finish breakfast slowly.

**Family.** [Daphne Ng](../09/08-the-other-side-of-the-cove.md#ah-r1272) is Nathan’s parent’s cousin. Nathan puts the kettle on. Daphne chooses the music. The cups come out before either brings up the reason for the visit. They trust a difficult conversation more if it has had an ordinary beginning. Asked about *The other side of the cove*, Daphne says, “I gave the money after selling my second company.”

**People in this story.** [Ethan Anand](#ah-r0734) (care colleague). The carer who stays for breakfast finally hears the version of the client known on afternoons.

**Personality.** Nathan finds it easier to own up to a mistake than to accept praise. The worse the mistake, the more precise the account of exactly when it happened.

**Appearance.** Nathan has a small notch in the left eyebrow. Look for black hair twisted into a small knot. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a dark green knitted vest with a pen clipped inside the neckline. In conversation Nathan gives a little nod before asking a hard question.

**Under pressure**

- Two carers turn up for one visit. Nathan asks the client which ordinary task would be easier with two people present.
- A stranger waves a phone camera in a crying person's face. Nathan offers an ordinary errand that lets the distressed person leave without being followed by an audience.
- When thanked for a shared success, Nathan points to the person who noticed the original mistake.

**People in this story.** [Sam Rose](#ah-r0738). An old diploma goes on the wall during the overlap the accountant is examining.

**Under pressure**
