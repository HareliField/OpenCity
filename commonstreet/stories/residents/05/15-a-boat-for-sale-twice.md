## Sasha Wong

AH-R0741 · Age 52 · Separated boat co-owner · Home — the streets inland from Pier Beach

**Life in the city.** “I still remember signing a purchase while my partner wrote the boat's name.” On Thursday, near Maré Coffee, Sasha likes the table where people coming off shift stop checking the time.

**In their own words.** “I wrote my ex's number the first time. We haven't decided which of us owns the good years aboard.”

**What Sasha wants.** To agree who actually handles the sale.

**Family.** [Philip Yu](../09/03-the-million-dollar-flat.md#ah-r1223) is Sasha’s cousin. Sasha offers the first invitation. Philip gets to change the plan. They learned that a promise to visit is easier to keep when changing a time does not count as letting down the whole family. Asked about *The million-dollar flat*, Philip says, “I am the son who asked for an advance against the inheritance.”

**People in this story.** [Lennon Ahmed](#ah-r0750) (adult child). The parent who wrote the wrong number finally gets the same question answered by the child.

**Personality.** Sasha prefers exact instructions at work and pleasant surprises at home. The two standards occasionally collide, especially when a relative thinks dropping by counts as notice.

**Appearance.** Sasha is easy to recognize by a loose dark braid and a wide brow and a narrow jaw. Usually dressed in a cropped bottle-green coat with a knotted piece of sail thread on a key ring, Sasha gathers both sleeves neatly before settling in when listening.

**Under pressure**

- The boat's owners give two answers about selling. Sasha asks both owners which decision has actually been made.
- An unfamiliar person asks for directions but cannot read the street signs. Sasha draws the route by its turns and entrances and has the person show which turn comes first.
- When a visitor arrives unexpectedly, Sasha clears one chair and says honestly how long the visit can last.
