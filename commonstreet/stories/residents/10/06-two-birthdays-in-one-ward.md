# Two birthdays in one ward

[When the lights go out](README.md) · [City directory](../directory.md)

## Ines Baines

AH-R1401 · Age 73 · Retired ward worker · Home — the streets inland from Pier Beach

**Life in the city.** “I still remember playing cards for buttons in the ward linen room.” People around Paper & Pine Books know Ines by a small Friday habit. Ines starts with the handwritten shelf cards and often leaves with a suggestion for somebody else.

**In their own words.** “I am old enough that a surprise party feels like somebody rehearsing my funeral. I want a hand of cards.”

**What Ines wants.** To play the requested birthday hand of cards.

**Family.** [Javier Anders](../03/12-one-unposted-detour.md#ah-r0415) is Ines’s younger relative by marriage. Ines can always ask for five minutes alone. Javier asks before bringing somebody along. It keeps a conversation between two people from unexpectedly turning into a family committee. Asked about *One unposted detour*, Javier says, “I borrowed a bicycle to deliver the last parcel.”

**People in this story.** [Daphne Perez](#ah-r1404). The retired worker's chosen birthday includes playing cards with the old supervisor.

**Personality.** Ines is patient with a person learning and fiercely impatient with somebody pretending to know. A half-finished job left where a beginner can see it is an invitation, as far as Ines is concerned.

**Appearance.** Ines is easy to recognize by gray hair trimmed very close behind the ears and a strong profile and a little space between the front teeth. Usually dressed in a charcoal zip cardigan with a tiny red thread tied through the lowest buttonhole, Ines draws breath as if to speak and lets someone else go first when listening.

**Under pressure**

- One birthday patient wants a quiet ward. Ines lets the quiet patient choose a low-key celebration separate from the party.
- A frightened child is waiting outside a shuttered shop. Ines stays where the child can see the shop door and asks the neighboring counter to help find the adult expected there.
- When someone invents an answer, Ines asks them to try it in front of the person who will have to live with it.

## Bailey Salcedo

AH-R1403 · Age 42 · Hospital party volunteer · Home — the western residential streets

**In their own words.** “I bought matching cakes before asking either patient which flavors they eat.”

**What Bailey wants.** To replace one matched cake with two chosen flavors.

**Family.** [Alexei Gross](../05/14-the-split-visit.md#ah-r0733) is Bailey’s older cousin. Bailey brings something that can still be enjoyed tomorrow. Alexei checks the date twice. **People in this story.** [Una Bell](#ah-r1409). The cake volunteer can hold a slice after learning when the cleaner arrives.

**Appearance.** Bailey has a flat bridge to the nose and a mobile brow. Look for a smooth side part with one rebellious curl. Out of work clothes, the familiar outfit is a sand-colored linen shirt with a fabric zip pull cut from a school scarf. In conversation Bailey holds eye contact until the other person has finished.

**Under pressure**

- One birthday patient wants a quiet ward. Bailey lets the quiet patient choose a low-key celebration separate from the party.
- A customer is short at the till while a queue forms. Bailey keeps the customer's place while they check the amount and gets the impatient people talking about something else.
- If a family plan changes at the door, Bailey keeps a place for the late person without making the others postpone their meal.

**Under pressure**
