# The empty chair on nights

[The families who come back](README.md) · [City directory](../directory.md)

**St. Mary Hospital.** A St. Mary night-shift nurse resigns suddenly. Her colleagues argue about whether to hold her farewell or leave her alone.

## Javier Aguilar

AH-R0951 · Age 39 · Ward nurse · Home — the neighborhood around Seabright Court

**Life in the city.** “I still remember leaving a sick job without a goodbye cup.” Thursday often includes a stop at Studio Boulevard, where Javier waits by the window where the whole crossing can be seen without leaning into the road.

**In their own words.** “I want the farewell because last time I left work sick nobody even phoned.”

**What Javier wants.** To give the former nurse a farewell she can freely decline.

**Family.** [Lily Wilcox](../02/15-a-sail-cut-small.md#ah-r0292) is Javier’s older cousin. Javier is allowed to ask about the bad news afterward. Lily calls about good news first. Neither wants every family conversation to begin with a bill, a diagnosis or an apology. Asked about *A sail cut small*, Lily says, “I wanted the stained piece because my niece spilled her first cup of tea there in a swell.”

**People in this story.** [Rosa Taylor](#ah-r0957). They decide which farewells a neighbor can actually deliver without violating a confidence.

**Personality.** Javier tells a better story in a kitchen than at a microphone. Family members sometimes underestimate how far Javier will go to back a quiet opinion once it is actually challenged.

**Appearance.** Javier is easy to recognize by auburn hair cut square at the nape and two dimples that appear only with a full smile. Usually dressed in a mustard canvas overshirt with a pen clipped inside the neckline, Javier folds a paper napkin into quarters without noticing when listening.

**Under pressure**

- A replacement nurse receives the permanent badge. Javier welcomes the new nurse and saves the old worker's goodbye for a separate conversation.
- A driver splashes a market worker and tries to laugh it off. Javier offers the worker something dry first and leaves it to them whether to pursue the apology.
- If a relative makes a joke at a child's expense, Javier repeats the child's actual point and waits for a reply to that.

## Billie Hunt

AH-R0952 · Age 45 · Clinical receptionist · Home — the neighborhood around Seabright Court

**Life in the city.** “I still remember visiting someone who asked me to leave my badge in the car.” People around Maré Coffee know Billie by a small Wednesday habit. Billie likes the table where people coming off shift stop checking the time.

**In their own words.** “I know why she left. It's her marriage and her choice to tell the ward if she wants.”

**What Billie wants.** To keep the reason for leaving confidential.

**Family.** [Emilia Dean](../03/07-overdue-by-a-lifetime.md#ah-r0362) is Billie’s cousin. Billie chooses whether to use it. Emilia saves a place. The invitation means something because declining it has never ended the friendship between their households. Asked about *Overdue by a lifetime*, Emilia says, “My mother signed the slip. It's the earliest sample I've seen of the married name she later gave up.”

**People in this story.** [Mateo Katz](#ah-r0960). A breakfast invitation can reach the departed nurse without explaining her marriage to the ward.

**Personality.** Billie is fond of ambitious ideas and deeply suspicious of grand openings. The question Billie asks at any launch is who has the keys for cleaning up a month later.

**Appearance.** Billie is easy to recognize by a loose dark braid and a strong profile and a little space between the front teeth. Usually dressed in a faded indigo overshirt with a narrow brass watch set a little fast, Billie presses the lips together to keep a smile in when listening.

**Under pressure**

- The departing nurse asks for an ordinary breakfast. Billie keeps the breakfast small and sets work questions aside.
- Someone offers a cash reward for naming a rumored culprit. Billie asks what evidence supports the accusation and declines to supply a name on gossip alone.
- If offered a ceremonial role, Billie accepts only after finding out whether the person doing the regular work can come too.

## Lara Moss

AH-R0953 · Age 36 · Shift nurse · Home — Eastside

**Life in the city.** “I still remember forgetting a promise to my daughter during a double shift.” On Sunday, near the blocks below Foundry Lofts, Lara checks the little hand-lettered notices around Harbour Supply.

**In their own words.** “I covered four shifts and I am tired of being generous in ways that make me unreliable at home.”

**What Lara wants.** To protect time at home after extra shifts.

**Family.** [Maria Kent](../08/02-land-without-cranes.md#ah-r1067) is Lara’s cousin. Lara brings the paper and a pen. Maria decides what stays off the paper. They have helped each other make decisions that were far easier to discuss once the figures could be moved around. The decisions themselves still belong to the person asking. Asked about *Land without cranes*, Maria says, “I make lunch for sixteen day laborers there.”

**People in this story.** [Parker Anders](#ah-r0959). A worker covering repeated shifts meets the one who is finally asking for leave.

**Personality.** Lara is firm about boundaries and unusually forgiving of somebody who admits to crossing one. Demands to act grateful for unwanted help can end a friendship.

**Appearance.** Lara has a narrow face with an expressive upper lip. Look for a soft fringe that falls across one eyebrow. Out of work clothes, the familiar outfit is a mustard canvas overshirt with one loose pocket corner secured with green thread. In conversation Lara listens with the head tilted a little to the left.

**Under pressure**

- A replacement nurse receives the permanent badge. Lara welcomes the new nurse and saves the old worker's goodbye for a separate conversation.
- Smoke appears under a shop door after closing. Lara alerts the nearest occupied shop, stays outside and writes down when the smoke first appeared.
- If a relative arrives with a solution nobody requested, Lara thanks them for coming and explains the part that still belongs to the household.

## Victor Armas

**In their own words.** “I borrowed her spare trainers on my first night. I want to return them with the eight years still visible.”

**What Victor wants.** To return the trainers in person.

**Family.** [Lena Price](../06/04-the-saturday-swap.md#ah-r0790) is Victor’s half-sibling. Victor can always ask for five minutes alone. Lena asks before bringing somebody along. It keeps a conversation between two people from unexpectedly turning into a family committee. Asked about *The Saturday swap*, Lena says, “I agreed to work half the shift so my neighbor could take her citizenship oath.”

**People in this story.** [Gale Felix](#ah-r0958). The loaned shoes and the surplus cake are two practical thank-yous with very different recipients.

**Personality.** Victor loves a well-told anecdote and distrusts the neat ending. The parts Victor remembers are the interruptions, unfinished jobs and people who had to go back to work halfway through.

**Appearance.** Victor is easy to recognize by a swept fringe and a closely clipped nape and a narrow face with an expressive upper lip. Usually dressed in a plum-colored cardigan with a comb in a sleeve sewn into the lining, Victor leans a shoulder against the wall during long conversations when listening.

**Under pressure**

- The departing nurse asks for an ordinary breakfast. Victor keeps the breakfast small and sets work questions aside.
- A driver splashes a market worker and tries to laugh it off. Victor offers the worker something dry first and leaves it to them whether to pursue the apology.
- When invited to tell the story later, Victor includes the name of the person who arrived after the photograph.

## Nina Arendt

AH-R0955 · Age 54 · Nurse educator · Home — Old Town

**Life in the city.** “I still remember training a night nurse on the longest morning of my life.” People around Harbour Bakery know Nina by a small Sunday habit. Nina buys one familiar thing and asks who chose the unfamiliar thing in the window.

**In their own words.** “I trained her and missed every sign she was working past her limit.”

**What Nina wants.** To ask the nurse which signs the educator missed.

**Family.** [Penelope Tang](../08/10-the-first-delivery-fee.md#ah-r1141) is Nina’s younger cousin. Nina remembers the birthdays. Penelope asks what the birthday guest would like this year. A present that was perfect at sixteen may be completely wrong at forty. They take the trouble of finding out. Asked about *The first delivery fee*, Penelope says, “I volunteered because the route got me out of a miserable house.”

**People in this story.** [Ashley Brooks](#ah-r0956) (mentee). The educator lets the new nurse discuss the promotion without calling the former nurse replaceable.

**Personality.** Nina has excellent recall for voices and a poor memory for official titles. Someone who waited patiently at a counter years ago is more likely to be remembered than the person in charge.

**Appearance.** Out of work clothes, look for a charcoal zip cardigan and a fabric zip pull cut from a school scarf. Nina has a long face and high cheekbones, with short copper curls. Nina draws small circles on a tabletop while waiting during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- A replacement nurse receives the permanent badge. Nina welcomes the new nurse and saves the old worker's goodbye for a separate conversation.
- Somebody finds a lost wallet outside the bank. Nina asks the counter staff for a receipt so its return can be traced even after their shift changes.
- When somebody trades on a title, Nina asks for the name of the person who will still be around tomorrow.

## Ashley Brooks

AH-R0956 · Age 30 · New permanent nurse · Home — Eastside

**Life in the city.** “I still remember receiving a promotion nobody in the room wanted to celebrate.” On Thursday, near the public road outside the Port of Asphalt, Ashley can recognize shift change by the lull between two groups of walkers.

**In their own words.** “I wish somebody would ask how it feels to be the person who gets the permanent post she left.”

**What Ashley wants.** To acknowledge the loss while taking the new post.

**Family.** [Olivia Farrell](../06/14-care-with-a-clock.md#ah-r0882) is Ashley’s second cousin. Ashley brings something that can still be enjoyed tomorrow. Olivia checks the date twice. Arrangements do change in their family. Taking a delay seriously without treating it as an insult took both of them years. Asked about *Care with a clock*, Olivia says, “I shaved minutes off my notes until my own pay packet came up short.”

**People in this story.** [Nina Arendt](#ah-r0955) (nurse educator). The educator lets the new nurse discuss the promotion without calling the former nurse replaceable.

**Personality.** Ashley finds it easier to own up to a mistake than to accept praise. The worse the mistake, the more precise the account of exactly when it happened.

**Appearance.** Ashley is easy to recognize by brown hair pinned away from the temples and a round face with a pronounced chin. Usually dressed in a gray fisher's sweater with a cloth patch so neatly matched it only shows in strong light, Ashley turns fully toward the person speaking when listening.

**Under pressure**

- The departing nurse asks for an ordinary breakfast. Ashley keeps the breakfast small and sets work questions aside.
- A stranger offers money to cut ahead of an exhausted neighbor. Ashley tells the stranger when the next opening is, then stays with the neighbor until their turn comes.
- When thanked for a shared success, Ashley points to the person who noticed the original mistake.

## Rosa Taylor

AH-R0957 · Age 41 · Pharmacy cleaner · Home — the streets inland from Pier Beach

**Life in the city.** “I still remember sitting in a neighbor's kitchen while the whole ward rang her doorbell.” On Wednesday, near the corner by Juniper Tailor, Rosa looks at the things people actually bring in for repair before looking in the display window.

**In their own words.** “I am her neighbor. I can deliver soup but I refuse to carry gossip in either direction.”

**What Rosa wants.** To deliver a meal without relaying gossip.

**Family.** [Ariel Jain](../06/03-money-after-marriage.md#ah-r0774) is Rosa’s second cousin. Rosa gets to change the plan. Ariel offers the first invitation. They learned that a promise to visit is easier to keep when changing a time does not count as letting down the whole family. Asked about *Money after marriage*, Ariel says, “I am the child who loves both parents and has no interest in inheriting their chair at the bank.”

**People in this story.** [Javier Aguilar](#ah-r0951). They decide which farewells a neighbor can actually deliver without violating a confidence.

**Personality.** Rosa keeps other people's confidences so well that friends sometimes mistake discretion for taking sides. Rosa wants to be trusted and resents being asked for proof of it.

**Appearance.** Rosa is easy to recognize by auburn hair cut square at the nape and a small notch in the left eyebrow. Usually dressed in a mustard canvas overshirt with a tiny red thread tied through the lowest buttonhole, Rosa squares up pieces of paper while thinking when listening.

**Under pressure**

- A replacement nurse receives the permanent badge. Rosa welcomes the new nurse and saves the old worker's goodbye for a separate conversation.
- A customer is short at the till while a queue forms. Rosa asks the cashier quietly which item can be set aside without making the customer explain their finances to the line.
- When somebody asks who said it first, Rosa offers to introduce them to the person willing to put a name to it.

They tend to get further with an important subject when it arrives in the middle of talk about dinner or work. Asked about *A summer marriage*, Lucia says, “I promised a sea view and the fog came down.”

**People in this story.** [Victor Armas](#ah-r0954). The loaned shoes and the surplus cake are two practical thank-yous with very different recipients.

**Appearance.** Away from the heat of the kitchen, look for an old plum windbreaker and a cloth patch so neatly matched it only shows in strong light. Gale has a narrow face with an expressive upper lip, with short copper curls. Gale looks down for a moment before saying something difficult during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- The departing nurse asks for an ordinary breakfast. Gale keeps the breakfast small and sets work questions aside.
- An envelope of wages is found under a café chair. Gale brings the envelope to the counter unopened and stays long enough to make a note of who accepted it.

## Parker Anders

AH-R0959 · Age 38 · Night porter · Home — the neighborhood around Seabright Court

**Life in the city.** “I still remember asking for leave after watching somebody else disappear.” Wednesday often includes a stop at the entrance to the Wash House, where Parker stops for local notices even on a day without a load to wash.

**In their own words.** “I voted against the farewell. Her leaving made me realize I need to ask for leave myself.”

**What Parker wants.** To request leave before reaching the same limit.

**Family.** [Jack Gomez](../05/02-the-crane-shadow.md#ah-r0620) is Parker’s second cousin. Parker brings whatever was actually growing or affordable that week. Jack makes room at the table. The meal sometimes produces a solution. Even when it does not, everyone goes away knowing what the other household is up against. Asked about *The crane shadow*, Jack says, “I booked a wedding picture at that exact spot.”

**People in this story.** [Lara Moss](#ah-r0953). A worker covering repeated shifts meets the one who is finally asking for leave.

**Personality.** Parker has a talent for getting adversaries to laugh at exactly the same thing. Parker knows better than to expect the laugh to settle what they are arguing about.

**Appearance.** Parker has black hair twisted into a small knot, a crooked front tooth visible when laughing, and a favorite piece of clothing, a washed violet jersey. It usually goes with a stitched row of blue crosses along an old repair. When somebody else has the floor, Parker squares up pieces of paper while thinking.

**Under pressure**

- A replacement nurse receives the permanent badge. Parker welcomes the new nurse and saves the old worker's goodbye for a separate conversation.
- A coworker arrives late after a night at the hospital. Parker writes down the shift time accurately, then helps them explain the circumstances without having to tell the whole room.
- When people declare an argument over too soon, Parker asks them to describe one concrete thing that will be different tomorrow.

## Mateo Katz

AH-R0960 · Age 40 · Breakfast cook · Home — the blocks north of the port

**Life in the city.** “I still remember breakfast in daylight after working beneath fluorescent strips.” On Friday, near Studio Boulevard, Mateo waits by the window where the whole crossing can be seen without leaning into the road.

**In their own words.** “I want one quiet breakfast with her outside work. She used to know how I took my eggs.”

**What Mateo wants.** To have breakfast away from the ward.

**Family.** [Adrian Keller](../10/15-the-last-light-at-the-harbor.md#ah-r1493) is Mateo’s second cousin. Mateo brings a spare shopping bag. Adrian chooses whether they walk home or sit a while. That small arrangement gives both a reason to see each other on weeks when asking for company outright feels difficult. Asked about *The last light at the harbor*, Adrian says, “I have been photographing glamorous boats. The owner asked me to photograph the little walk from deck to door instead.”

**People in this story.** [Billie Hunt](#ah-r0952). A breakfast invitation can reach the departed nurse without explaining her marriage to the ward.

**Personality.** Mateo enjoys bargaining, including over things already settled. It is partly a joke and partly a test of whether the other person feels able to say no.

**Appearance.** Mateo has a wide brow and a narrow jaw. Look for short sandy hair and a marked widow's peak. Away from the heat of the kitchen, the familiar outfit is a faded indigo overshirt with a folded shopping list secured by a miniature clip. In conversation Mateo raises one eyebrow long before saying yes.

**Under pressure**

- The departing nurse asks for an ordinary breakfast. Mateo keeps the breakfast small and sets work questions aside.
- A cyclist goes down in a crowded crossing. Mateo moves the onlookers' bags out of the way, asks the rider what hurts, and asks one named bystander to call for help.
- When a friend starts agreeing too quickly, Mateo puts the deal away and suggests a walk.
