## Lee Ingram

**Life in the city.** “I still remember seeing my damaged car in a newspaper box.” On Friday, near Lighthouse Cove, Lee takes a few minutes by the low rocks, where conversation sounds different as the tide turns.

**In their own words.** “I survived it. I need one route to work where nobody recognizes me from the newspaper photograph.”

**Family.** [Darcy Baran](../03/08-the-painted-footprint.md#ah-r0378) is Lee’s stepsibling. Lee can stop the walk at any corner. Darcy chooses the familiar route. Walking side by side makes it easier to disagree without turning each disagreement into a contest. Asked about *The painted footprint*, Darcy says, “I'm measuring everyone's guesses for a statistics class.”

**People in this story.** [Roland Pierce](#ah-r1016). The survivor can choose whether a picture should mark a road they must travel every day. Also in the story is [Manuel Peters](#ah-r1013) (former collision counterpart).

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a sand-colored linen shirt and a square of clean cloth for polishing smudges. Lee has full cheeks and a mouth that pulls slightly to one side, with black hair twisted into a small knot. Lee rubs a thumb along the edge of the nearest pocket during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a striped cotton shirt with a fabric zip pull cut from a school scarf. **Under pressure**
