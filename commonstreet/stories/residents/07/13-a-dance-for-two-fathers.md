## Agnes Lin

AH-R1021 · Age 18 · Graduating student · Home — the neighborhood around Market Flats

**Life in the city.** “I still remember learning a dance step against two different kitchen cabinets.” Thursday often includes a stop at Breakwater Way, where Agnes stops on the landward side of the cycle track to watch the first boats turn for home.

**In their own words.** “I am the graduate. I choreographed a turn that leaves each man momentarily dancing with the other.”

**What Agnes wants.** To dance with both fathers on chosen terms.

**Family.** [Norman Armas](../02/11-one-sleeve-short.md#ah-r0251) is Agnes’s first cousin once removed. Agnes chooses the music. Norman puts the kettle on. The cups come out before either brings up the reason for the visit. They trust a difficult conversation more if it has had an ordinary beginning. Asked about *One sleeve short*, Norman says, “I interview people at a desk. I never realized I only see their top half until I saw the chalk lines.”

**People in this story.** [Faye Tran](#ah-r1026). The choreographer and dance teacher work out where each father has room to move. Other connections include [Tom Moreno](#ah-r1022) (stepfather), [Desmond Monroe](#ah-r1023) (birth father).

**Personality.** Agnes can be wonderfully decisive about an emergency and maddeningly undecided about a gift. This is because a person can say precisely what hurts and rarely says exactly what they want.

**Appearance.** Agnes has short, uneven waves that refuse to lie flat, a softly rounded chin under sharp cheekbones, and a favorite piece of clothing, a black overshirt with pearl buttons. It usually goes with a pocket notebook shaped by years of sitting on it. When somebody else has the floor, Agnes presses the lips together to keep a smile in.

**Under pressure**
