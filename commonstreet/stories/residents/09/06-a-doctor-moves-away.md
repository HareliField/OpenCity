# A doctor moves away

## Riley Macrae

AH-R1251 · Age 54 · Departing hospital physician · Home — the streets around Foundry Lofts

**Life in the city.** “I still remember waiting for a parent to finish breakfast before asking about pain.” Wednesday often includes a stop at the brick passage off the shop row, where Riley takes the longer way when the smell from the ovens reaches the pavement.

**In their own words.** “I asked my patients what mattered for years. My father asked me the same question at his kitchen sink.”

**What Riley wants.** To move close enough to see the father each week.

**Family.** [Hamish James](../05/05-the-clean-ledger.md#ah-r0642) is Riley’s half-sibling. Riley calls back when there is finally a little privacy. Hamish leaves the first voice message. In their family a missed ring can mean a bus, a customer, a sleeping child or a bad hour. Neither treats it as an answer in itself. Asked about *The clean ledger*, Hamish says, “I bought the man's tools and lost my shop anyway.”

**People in this story.** [Nadine Lam](#ah-r1257). The doctor and the father's carer settle what returning as a daughter will mean at home. Also in the story is [Sam Lim](#ah-r1253) (succeeding physician).

**Personality.** Riley is firm about boundaries and unusually forgiving of somebody who admits to crossing one. Demands to act grateful for unwanted help can end a friendship.

**Appearance.** Riley is easy to recognize by a dark undercut with a longer wave on top and a flat bridge to the nose and a mobile brow. Usually dressed in a rust-colored canvas coat with a partly rubbed-away embroidered initial, Riley presses the lips together to keep a smile in when listening.

**Under pressure**

- A patient asks the leaving doctor to stay one more month. Riley asks what handover would help the patient if the move date stands.
- A family supper turns into an argument about old debt. Riley asks each person to say what they believe is actually owed before anybody proposes a payment.
- If a relative arrives with a solution nobody requested, Riley thanks them for coming and explains the part that still belongs to the household.

## Bailey Moran

AH-R1252 · Age 73 · Retired school bursar · Home — the neighborhood above Lighthouse Cove

**Life in the city.** “I still remember having to tell my history twice in the same waiting room.” On Friday, near the entrance to the Wash House, Bailey stops for local notices even on a day without a load to wash.

**In their own words.** “I have no claim on her evenings and want an introduction to the person who will read what she wrote about me.”

**What Bailey wants.** To get a personal introduction to the successor.

**Family.** [Vincent Farah](../02/02-five-missing-lunches.md#ah-r0162) is Bailey’s younger cousin. Bailey calls back when there is finally a little privacy. Vincent leaves the first voice message. In their family a missed ring can mean a bus, a customer, a sleeping child or a bad hour. Neither treats it as an answer in itself. Asked about *Five missing lunches*, Vincent says, “My son painted a whale on the lid. It's the only thing he drew before his hand operation.”

**People in this story.** [Sam Lim](#ah-r1253). The longtime patient meets the successor while the departing physician can still make the introduction.

**Personality.** Bailey loves a well-told anecdote and distrusts the neat ending. The parts Bailey remembers are the interruptions, unfinished jobs and people who had to go back to work halfway through.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a cream pullover and a copper-colored clasp that has been repaired by hand. Bailey has full cheeks and a mouth that pulls slightly to one side, with salt-and-pepper curls. Bailey raises one eyebrow long before saying yes during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- The incoming doctor is compared with the old one. Bailey gives the new doctor a chance to speak before retelling the old doctor's habits.
- An unfamiliar person asks for directions but cannot read the street signs. Bailey walks with them as far as the first landmark and points to the next one from there.
- When invited to tell the story later, Bailey includes the name of the person who arrived after the photograph.

**Appearance.** Sam has a flat bridge to the nose and a mobile brow. Look for short sandy hair and a marked widow's peak. Out of work clothes, the familiar outfit is a plum-colored cardigan with one loose pocket corner secured with green thread. In conversation Sam takes off the watch before a long conversation.

**Under pressure**

- A patient asks the leaving doctor to stay one more month. Sam asks what handover would help the patient if the move date stands.

## Marin Lim

AH-R1256 · Age 58 · Court typist · Home — Old Town

**Life in the city.** “I still remember writing a careful letter to someone I never much liked.” People around the market by Market Flats know Marin by a small Friday habit. Marin often waits until the delivery baskets are stacked before going in.

**In their own words.** “I never liked her manner and liked her thoroughness. The letter I wrote took both things into account.”

**What Marin wants.** To deliver an honest letter of appreciation.

**Family.** [Gareth Yeo](../03/05-rent-in-a-biscuit-tin.md#ah-r0345) is Marin’s first cousin once removed. Marin can stop the walk at any corner. Gareth chooses the familiar route. Walking side by side makes it easier to disagree without turning each disagreement into a contest. Asked about *Rent in a biscuit tin*, Gareth says, “I wanted the money counted in public. Then I learned somebody was saving to leave home quietly.”

**People in this story.** [Jay Arendt](#ah-r1258). Precise criticism and careful interpreting both make for better handover notes than indiscriminate praise.

**Personality.** Marin always notices who paid for the last round. In a group that can be a kindness or a small tyranny, and Marin is trying to tell the difference before the bill arrives.

**Appearance.** Marin has a flat bridge to the nose and a mobile brow. Look for a soft fringe that falls across one eyebrow. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a pale blue work shirt with a train token tied to a zipper pull. In conversation Marin listens with the head tilted a little to the left.

**Under pressure**

- The incoming doctor is compared with the old one. Marin gives the new doctor a chance to speak before retelling the old doctor's habits.

- If somebody tries to repay an old favor all at once, Marin suggests a smaller gesture they can repeat without hardship.
