# Contribution website

The site uses `index.html`, `city.css`, `city.js`, clips, and [contribute.txt](contribute.txt). Work here starts with the [contribution-site design patterns](../../docs/design-system.md#contribution-site) or the [collaboration workflow](../../docs/collaboration.md#how-contributions-work), depending on the change.

- Keep the copy-ready prompt in `contribute.txt` and verify both copying it and opening its readable fallback.
- Keep the summary of city rules aligned with the [full rulebook](../../docs/collaboration.md#city-rulebook). Preserve the preview and submission approval boundary in the prompt.
- Use approved city footage with clear playback controls. Check navigation, keyboard focus and the layout at narrow and wide sizes when changing the site.
