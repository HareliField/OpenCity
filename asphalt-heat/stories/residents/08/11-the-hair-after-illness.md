# The hair after illness

[Who gets to decide](README.md) · [City directory](../directory.md)

## Robin Gomes

AH-R1151 · Age 44 · Recovering client and sculptor · Home — the streets inland from Pier Beach

**Life in the city.** “I still remember watching my old hair collect in the hospital basin.” Wednesday often includes a stop at Ferris Deli, where Robin asks for the end slice and listens to the names the counter workers use for regulars.

**In their own words.** “I wanted my old photograph. In the chair I saw how much I like this new cowlick.”

**What Robin wants.** To choose a haircut that honors the new texture.

**Family.** [Talia Carvalho](../04/12-the-ladder-crossing.md#ah-r0566) is Robin’s half-sibling. Robin chooses whether to use it. Talia saves a place. The invitation means something because declining it has never ended the friendship between their households. Asked about *The ladder crossing*, Talia says, “I balanced my shopping on a rung while we waited.”

**People in this story.** [Ivy Arnett](#ah-r1153) (stylist). The client talks to the stylist about what to keep of the new texture.

**Personality.** Robin enjoys bargaining, including over things already settled. It is partly a joke and partly a test of whether the other person feels able to say no.

**Appearance.** Robin has a crooked front tooth visible when laughing. Look for a cropped cut that shows the shape of the ears. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a brown corduroy jacket with a pocket notebook shaped by years of sitting on it. In conversation Robin gives a little nod before asking a hard question.

**Under pressure**

- Someone asks to take a photograph before the haircut. Robin waits for the customer's permission before anyone takes a picture.
- Someone begins blaming the youngest worker for a missing item. Robin says what they know in their own name and refuses to treat a guess as something the child has to disprove.
- When a friend starts agreeing too quickly, Robin puts the deal away and suggests a walk.
