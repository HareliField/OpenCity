## Penelope Tang

AH-R1141 · Age 28 · Former volunteer and paid shop worker · Home — the streets around Foundry Lofts

**Life in the city.** “I still remember delivering lunch to avoid coming home too early.” Saturday often includes a stop at the market by Market Flats, where Penelope often waits until the delivery baskets are stacked before going in.

**In their own words.** “I volunteered because the route got me out of a miserable house. I can't give my new wages away.”

**What Penelope wants.** To start the paid job with a clean handover of deliveries.

**Family.** [Nina Arendt](../07/06-the-empty-chair-on-nights.md#ah-r0955) is Penelope’s older cousin. Penelope asks what the birthday guest would like this year. Nina remembers the birthdays. A present that was perfect at sixteen may be completely wrong at forty. They take the trouble of finding out. Asked about *The empty chair on nights*, Nina says, “I trained her and missed every sign she was working past her limit.”

**People in this story.** [Jonas Osborne](#ah-r1142). The former volunteer tells a regular who really paid for delivery time. Also in the story is [Erin Roche](#ah-r1149) (deli colleague).

**Personality.** Penelope carries anger into a conversation very slowly. Once it arrives, a quiet statement of facts can be considerably harder to sidestep than a shout.

**Appearance.** Penelope has a cropped cut that shows the shape of the ears, two dimples that appear only with a full smile, and a favorite piece of clothing, a charcoal zip cardigan. It usually goes with a pen clipped inside the neckline. When somebody else has the floor, Penelope draws breath as if to speak and lets someone else go first.

**Under pressure**

- A housebound customer cannot afford the delivery fee. Penelope asks whether combining a delivery with a willing neighbor would help.
- Somebody finds a lost wallet outside the bank. Penelope asks the counter staff for a receipt so its return can be traced even after their shift changes.
- If a friend asks for a false alibi for an ordinary missed appointment, Penelope offers to accompany them while they tell the truth.

**Appearance.** Frances has a round face with a pronounced chin. Look for brown hair pinned away from the temples. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a soft blue checked shirt with a thin silver ring worn on a cord. In conversation Frances turns fully toward the person speaking.

**Under pressure**

**Appearance.** Tom has a center part and straight chestnut hair, a strong profile and a little space between the front teeth, and a favorite piece of clothing, a narrow-striped sweater. It usually goes with a pen clipped inside the neckline. When somebody else has the floor, Tom leans a shoulder against the wall during long conversations.

**Under pressure**

- If offered a ceremonial role, Tom accepts only after finding out whether the person doing the regular work can come too.
