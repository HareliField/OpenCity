## Ismael Fong

AH-R0831 · Age 40 · Hotel night clerk · Home — the western residential streets

**Life in the city.** “I still remember waiting for my father beside a gate nobody used.” Tuesday often includes a stop at the blocks below Foundry Lofts, where Ismael checks the little hand-lettered notices around Harbour Supply.

**In their own words.** “I want to meet him at the gate so my mother can choose when to see him.”

**What Ismael wants.** To let the mother choose the moment of contact.

**Family.** [Bianca Moreau](../09/12-the-driving-lesson-fund.md#ah-r1314) is Ismael’s cousin. Ismael saves a place. Bianca chooses whether to use it. The invitation means something because declining it has never ended the friendship between their households. Asked about *The driving lesson fund*, Bianca says, “I voted to give the slot to a parent. The single person who lost gets up at four to visit her dad.”

**People in this story.** [Adriana Diaz](#ah-r0835) (child). One family welcome has to make room for both an adult's anger and a child's curiosity.

**Personality.** Ismael treats a small promise with the gravity others reserve for a contract. This makes Ismael lovely to rely on and surprisingly difficult to invite on a whim.

**Appearance.** Ismael has a crooked front tooth visible when laughing. Look for close-cropped dark curls. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is an old plum windbreaker with a copper-colored clasp that has been repaired by hand. In conversation Ismael leans a shoulder against the wall during long conversations.

**Under pressure**

- The returning father wants a family photograph immediately. Ismael asks each household member privately whether they want to be in the picture.
- Someone begins blaming the youngest worker for a missing item. Ismael asks everybody to retrace their own last ordinary actions before asking the young worker a question.
- When offered a favor with ambiguous terms, Ismael says what can be returned and asks whether that will do.

## Anita Abbott

AH-R0832 · Age 43 · Child care manager · Home — the western residential streets

**Life in the city.** “I still remember the winter we postponed three dental appointments.” Friday often includes a stop at the courtyard by Ferry Street Rooms, where Anita prefers the bench where the sound of the shop door reaches before the visitor does.

**In their own words.** “I forgave the man years ago. I am still furious about the rent he left and the teeth we couldn't afford to fix.”

**What Anita wants.** To name the old rent loss without forbidding the visit.

**Family.** [Gilbert Moss](../10/13-one-last-dress-rehearsal.md#ah-r1476) is Anita’s half-sibling. Anita picks the meeting place. Gilbert sets the time to leave. It lets them stay close without turning a family visit into the loss of an entire day. They have both had stretches when that distinction mattered. Asked about *One last dress rehearsal*, Gilbert says, “I sewed the costumes. This was the first audience member who noticed we had put real pockets in all the work clothes.”

**People in this story.** [Jeff Jones](#ah-r0839). The relative who paid the fare admits a hope the relative who paid the back rent already understands.

**Personality.** Anita is good at starting things, dreadful at waiting for them to take effect. Close family can tell how worried Anita is by the number of tiny errands suddenly being done.

**Appearance.** Anita has a high puff tied with plain cord, a narrow face with an expressive upper lip, and a favorite piece of clothing, a striped cotton shirt. It usually goes with a seam let out by a narrow strip of contrasting fabric. When somebody else has the floor, Anita holds eye contact until the other person has finished.

**Under pressure**

- A sibling leaves the room during the welcome dinner. Anita leaves the departing sibling room to get out without turning supper into a vote.
- A driver splashes a market worker and tries to laugh it off. Anita asks the driver to come back on foot and look at the damaged goods before discussing cost.
- When asked to wait without intervening, Anita agrees on a check-in time and keeps busy somewhere out of sight.

## Charlie Starr

AH-R0834 · Age 39 · Spare-room decorator · Home — the blocks north of the port

**Life in the city.** “I still remember painting a bedroom before I knew who its owner was.” People around the downtown fountain know Charlie by a small Monday habit. Charlie keeps a little time free to hear who is playing at the far end of the park.

**In their own words.** “I painted the spare room blue. He called it a color for a nursery and I nearly sent him packing.”

**What Charlie wants.** To finish the blue room on terms the returning parent can accept.

**Family.** [Rene Mott](../01/07-an-unfinished-atlas.md#ah-r0062) is Charlie’s cousin. Charlie can always ask for five minutes alone. Rene asks before bringing somebody along. It keeps a conversation between two people from unexpectedly turning into a family committee. Asked about *An unfinished atlas*, Rene says, “My hand shook the day I ruined a school register with too much water.”

**People in this story.** [Sonia Garza](#ah-r0837). Preparing a bedroom competes with the guest's own need for occasional space in the house.

**Personality.** Charlie is fond of ambitious ideas and deeply suspicious of grand openings. The question Charlie asks at any launch is who has the keys for cleaning up a month later.

**Appearance.** Charlie has two dimples that appear only with a full smile. Look for a long plait tucked inside the collar. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a pale blue work shirt with a watch on a cracked leather band. In conversation Charlie takes off the watch before a long conversation.

**Under pressure**

- A sibling leaves the room during the welcome dinner. Charlie leaves the departing sibling room to get out without turning supper into a vote.
- The last bus leaves while an elderly stranger is still crossing toward it. Charlie asks the stranger who will be expecting them, then helps find a way to tell that person.
- If offered a ceremonial role, Charlie accepts only after finding out whether the person doing the regular work can come too.

## Adriana Diaz

**Life in the city.** “I still remember learning which grandfather left-handedness came from.” Thursday often includes a stop at the public road outside the Port of Asphalt, where Adriana can recognize shift change by the lull between two groups of walkers.

**In their own words.** “I want my grandfather to teach me one thing with his hands before anybody asks him to explain.”

**What Adriana wants.** To learn one practical skill from the grandfather.

**Family.** [Oakley Carbone](../08/15-the-skipper-goes-ashore.md#ah-r1197) is Adriana’s older cousin. When the family split between two addresses, the good frying pan traveled with whoever cooked Sunday dinner. The pan has outlasted the quarrel and both kitchens. Adriana can see when a sensible rule has become an insult. Asked about *The skipper goes ashore*, Oakley says, “I was her first sailing student. I asked her to teach my child from the pier without pretending she was still on board.”

**People in this story.** [Ismael Fong](#ah-r0831) (parent). One family welcome has to make room for both an adult's anger and a child's curiosity.

**Personality.** Adriana is firm about boundaries and unusually forgiving of somebody who admits to crossing one. Demands to act grateful for unwanted help can end a friendship.

**Appearance.** Outside school, look for a boxy denim jacket and a little coil of soft measuring tape. Adriana has a flat bridge to the nose and a mobile brow, with thick, tightly coiled hair. Adriana counts on the side of one finger where hardly anybody notices during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- The returning father wants a family photograph immediately. Adriana asks each household member privately whether they want to be in the picture.
- A diner goes silent in the middle of a noisy family celebration. Adriana takes a small plate to the quieter end of the table and saves the seat beside it.
- If a relative arrives with a solution nobody requested, Adriana thanks them for coming and explains the part that still belongs to the household.
