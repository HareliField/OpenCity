## Alex Hamm

AH-R0881 · Age 71 · Retired engraver · Home — the western residential streets

**Life in the city.** “I still remember choosing a shirt for the first day of retirement.” People around Juniper Street know Alex by a small Thursday habit. Alex knows which of the west-side porches has a seat in the shade.

**In their own words.** “I can dress in sixteen minutes. I will not practice being ashamed of the sixteenth.”

**What Alex wants.** To take the sixteenth minute without apology.

**Family.** [Albert Bennett](../03/11-the-widow-s-razor.md#ah-r0402) is Alex’s cousin raised in the same household. Alex brings something that can still be enjoyed tomorrow. Albert checks the date twice. Arrangements do change in their family. Taking a delay seriously without treating it as an insult took both of them years. Asked about *The widow's razor*, Albert says, “I can sharpen it until it forgets its shape.”

**People in this story.** [Gwen Hooper](#ah-r0885). They discuss an easier fastening without assuming an old wedding shirt is disposable. Also in the story is [Martha Ueda](#ah-r0884) (adult child).

**Personality.** Alex keeps other people's confidences so well that friends sometimes mistake discretion for taking sides. Alex wants to be trusted and resents being asked for proof of it.

**Appearance.** Alex has a thin scattering of white hair over the crown, a round face with a pronounced chin, and a favorite piece of clothing, a soft blue checked shirt. It usually goes with a comb in a sleeve sewn into the lining. When somebody else has the floor, Alex raises one eyebrow long before saying yes.

**Under pressure**

- A visit runs into a second billing block. Alex records the extra time actually spent with the client.
- A friend asks for a loan due back before payday. Alex offers to cover one concrete expense and leaves the friend room to say the expense isn't the real problem.
- When somebody asks who said it first, Alex offers to introduce them to the person willing to put a name to it.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for an unlined tan jacket and a replacement button a shade darker than the others. Marcel has a long face and high cheekbones, with closely cropped salt-and-pepper hair. Marcel looks at people's hands before looking at what they carry during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- If someone else wins the opportunity, Marcel turns up the next morning with the one piece of advice that would have helped either of them.
