# The Saturday swap

[What a working week costs](README.md) · [City directory](../directory.md)

**The city police station.** Officers from the city department try to trade one Saturday watch without leaving a beat uncovered. Each has told family a different version of when they will be free.

## Samir Shah

AH-R0781 · Age 39 · Patrol officer · Home — Eastside · Department ID ah-pd-103 · Patrol, watch 1, badge 103

**Life in the city.** “I still remember a promised sports day ruined by my overtime.” People around the window at Night Shift Donuts know Samir by a small Tuesday habit. Samir knows when the going-home crowd and the coming-to-work crowd will both be there.

**In their own words.** “I promised to watch my son's first swim and never wrote the date down. He's stopped asking if I'm coming.”

**What Samir wants.** To keep a promise about the son's swim.

**Family.** [Finn Kahn](../07/10-an-old-restaurant-debt.md#ah-r0999) is Samir’s cousin. Samir chooses the familiar route. Finn can stop the walk at any corner. Walking side by side makes it easier to disagree without turning each disagreement into a contest. Asked about *An old restaurant debt*, Finn says, “I would use the returned money for repairs.”

**People in this story.** [Dev Cole](#ah-r0786). A promised swimming lesson and a childcare closing time leave very little slack in one watch swap.

**Personality.** Samir has a strong competitive streak and a very soft spot for the person finishing last. A prize tastes better when everybody had enough time to enter.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a pale blue work shirt and a replacement button a shade darker than the others. Samir has a straight nose and deep-set eyes, with auburn hair cut square at the nape. Samir starts to lift a hand, then waits to be invited to speak during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- A watch swap falls through overnight. Samir tells the households relying on the swap before looking for a replacement.
- A cyclist goes down in a crowded crossing. Samir stays with the rider after the people who saw the fall start drifting away.
- If accused of favoritism, Samir explains exactly what help was offered and offers the same practical help to the accuser.

**Appearance.** Ruth has a cropped cut that shows the shape of the ears, a long face and high cheekbones, and a favorite piece of clothing, a soft blue checked shirt. It usually goes with a pen clipped inside the neckline. When somebody else has the floor, Ruth leans a shoulder against the wall during long conversations.

**Under pressure**

- If told to back off, Ruth does so immediately and stays available at a distance the other person chooses.
