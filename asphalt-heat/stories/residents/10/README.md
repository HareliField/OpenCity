# When the lights go out

[All residents](../README.md) · [Alphabetical directory](../directory.md)

Residents 1351–1500. Each episode follows ten people who are affected differently by the same event.

| Episode | People | Opening situation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [A closing hour](01-a-closing-hour.md) | 1351–1360 | A Wash House operator who has never had a full day off decides to close for an ordinary family celebration. |
| [A last ship together](02-a-last-ship-together.md) | 1361–1370 | Two lifelong dock workers will retire a week apart. Their supervisors can offer them one final shared unloading shift or separate paid farewell days. |
| [The gift nobody wants](03-the-gift-nobody-wants.md) | 1371–1380 | A wealthy local offers to buy a whole Old Town building for an adult child who wants a modest business elsewhere. |
| [Names on the night wall](04-names-on-the-night-wall.md) | 1381–1390 | The city department makes a wall for staff to thank the person who makes coming to work possible. Almost every name belongs to somebody outside the station. |
| [The return of a witness](05-the-return-of-a-witness.md) | 1391–1400 | A former neighborhood witness returns to the brick passage to visit a relative after moving away from people she testified against. |
| [Two birthdays in one ward](06-two-birthdays-in-one-ward.md) | 1401–1410 | St. Mary discovers that a young patient and a retired ward worker share a birthday and very different wishes for the day. |
| [An empty dedication](07-an-empty-dedication.md) | 1411–1420 | A local novelist leaves an empty dedication page and invites the printers, caregivers and relatives who made the book possible to decide what it says. |
| [No lifeguard at dusk](08-no-lifeguard-at-dusk.md) | 1421–1430 | A Pier Beach lifeguard finishes the last official shift of the season while a regular swimmer waits on the steps, reluctant to let summer end. |
| [A house divided kindly](09-a-house-divided-kindly.md) | 1431–1440 | Two western neighbors want to join their front gardens after their children move away. Their deeds, family histories and tastes in flowers differ. |
| [The kitchen without a boss](10-the-kitchen-without-a-boss.md) | 1441–1450 | An ill Night Shift owner gives three workers one month to run the bakery together. She will taste the results only after she returns. |
| [An ordinary mirror](11-an-ordinary-mirror.md) | 1451–1460 | A Juniper client asks to buy the fitting mirror in which she has seen herself as a schoolgirl, a bride and a recent widow. |
| [Three minutes at the bridge](12-three-minutes-at-the-bridge.md) | 1461–1470 | A group of Coast Ridge commuters realizes a three-minute difference in shift start times would let them share rides for an entire winter. |
| [One last dress rehearsal](13-one-last-dress-rehearsal.md) | 1471–1480 | A park youth group agrees to perform once for the parent who works during every ticketed show. |
| [The last home visit](14-the-last-home-visit.md) | 1481–1490 | A home-care worker retires from the western route and asks each client to teach her successor one thing she always got wrong. |
| [The last light at the harbor](15-the-last-light-at-the-harbor.md) | 1491–1500 | The four South Shore boats put out their cabin lights one at a time at the end of an ordinary working Sunday. People on shore realize each extinguished light means someone has arrived home. |
