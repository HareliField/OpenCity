## Cecilia Woods

AH-R0651 · Age 48 · Retail cashier · Home — the neighborhood around Market Flats

**Life in the city.** “I still remember a father's shoes going unworn beside the stove.” People around the corner outside Coastal Barber know Cecilia by a small Saturday habit. Cecilia usually checks the board before the regulars give a more colorful account of the news.

**In their own words.** “I bought my dad those slippers after he fell. I visited three wards to find the missing right.”

**What Cecilia wants.** To recover the father's missing right slipper.

**Family.** [Gloria Smith](../02/15-a-sail-cut-small.md#ah-r0300) is Cecilia’s first cousin once removed. Cecilia brings whatever was actually growing or affordable that week. Gloria makes room at the table. The meal sometimes produces a solution. Even when it does not, everyone goes away knowing what the other household is up against. Asked about *A sail cut small*, Gloria says, “I kept a postage-stamp square. It fits inside the passport I could never afford when that boat was new.”

**People in this story.** [Miles Marks](#ah-r0652). A family looking for a right slipper meets the wearer who can pass on an unused left.

**Personality.** Cecilia treats a small promise with the gravity others reserve for a contract. This makes Cecilia lovely to rely on and surprisingly difficult to invite on a whim.

**Appearance.** Cecilia has fine brows over wide-set eyes. Look for a blunt cut just below the ears. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a pale blue work shirt with a braided bracelet with a single wooden bead. In conversation Cecilia presses the lips together to keep a smile in.

**Under pressure**

- A patient opens the bag and finds two left slippers. Cecilia sets out the pair and asks the discharge worker to check the right foot too.
- Smoke appears under a shop door after closing. Cecilia calls for emergency assistance from outside and waits to give the street number.
- When offered a favor with ambiguous terms, Cecilia says what can be returned and asks whether that will do.

## Miles Marks

AH-R0652 · Age 72 · Retired tram operator · Home — Midtown

**Life in the city.** “I still remember the shoe shop that let me buy a single left.” People around the corner by Juniper Tailor know Miles by a small Thursday habit. Miles looks at the things people actually bring in for repair before looking in the display window.

**In their own words.** “One was mine. My right foot has been gone for a year and I have a cupboard of usable lefts.”

**What Miles wants.** To offer unused left shoes to people who can use them.

**Family.** [Robin Poole](../09/14-the-missing-sunday.md#ah-r1338) is Miles’s younger relative by marriage. Miles makes the call. Robin is free to hang up first. Some of their best calls last less than a minute. A change of tone is sometimes all the news either one needs. Asked about *The missing Sunday*, Robin says, “I don't watch sport. I cooked soup loud enough in his kitchen for him to know I stayed through the match.”

**People in this story.** [Cecilia Woods](#ah-r0651). A family looking for a right slipper meets the wearer who can pass on an unused left.

**Personality.** Miles is good at starting things, dreadful at waiting for them to take effect. Close family can tell how worried Miles is by the number of tiny errands suddenly being done.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for an olive canvas jacket and a knotted piece of sail thread on a key ring. Miles has a broad nose and a deep dimple on one cheek, with iron-gray waves. Miles listens with the head tilted a little to the left during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- A visitor offers shoes that are far too large. Miles offers to look for a usable pair before anyone is asked to walk in loose shoes.
- An argument on the pavement makes a bystander feel unsafe. Miles puts distance between the frightened person and the argument and asks nearby staff for help.
- When asked to wait without intervening, Miles agrees on a check-in time and keeps busy somewhere out of sight.

**Under pressure**
