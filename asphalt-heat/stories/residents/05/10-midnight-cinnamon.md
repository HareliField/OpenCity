## Seth Roberts

AH-R0691 · Age 41 · Oven tender · Home — the neighborhood above Lighthouse Cove

**Life in the city.** “I still remember my dad scorching spice while explaining a divorce.” On Tuesday, near Studio Boulevard, Seth waits by the window where the whole crossing can be seen without leaning into the road.

**In their own words.** “I hated that holiday until the sack opened. My father used to toast the spice while apologizing.”

**What Seth wants.** To re-create the father's toasted spice for supper.

**Family.** [Morgan Day](../07/13-a-dance-for-two-fathers.md#ah-r1028) is Seth’s first cousin once removed. Seth asks before bringing somebody along. Morgan can always ask for five minutes alone. It keeps a conversation between two people from unexpectedly turning into a family committee. Asked about *A dance for two fathers*, Morgan says, “I wrote the school's old dance rule years ago.”

**People in this story.** [Terry Sullivan](#ah-r0693). Two cooks ask relatives which smell was missing from their remembered holiday food. Also in the story is [Devin Ames](#ah-r0697) (spice courier).

**Personality.** Seth has a long memory for unfair prices and a short one for small insults. Seth can get along perfectly well with an enemy provided the account between them is clear.

**Appearance.** Seth has black hair twisted into a small knot, a round face with a pronounced chin, and a favorite piece of clothing, a brown corduroy jacket. It usually goes with a partly rubbed-away embroidered initial. When somebody else has the floor, Seth gathers both sleeves neatly before settling in.

**Under pressure**

- The spice buyer wants the mistaken sack returned. Seth sets aside a sample and confirms which holiday orders depend on it.
- An argument on the pavement makes a bystander feel unsafe. Seth puts distance between the frightened person and the argument and asks nearby staff for help.
- If a friend borrows money, Seth agrees on an amount both can bear losing before any cash changes hands.

## Carlo Gil

AH-R0692 · Age 55 · Spice wholesaler · Home — the western residential streets

**Life in the city.** “I still remember packing the smallest parcels for the customers counting coins.” On Wednesday, near Breakwater Way, Carlo stops on the landward side of the cycle track to watch the first boats turn for home.

**In their own words.** “I sell cinnamon by weight. I am trying to price the little packets customers buy just to carry home.”

**What Carlo wants.** To sell small scent packets at a fair price.

**Family.** [Walter Ray](../09/08-the-other-side-of-the-cove.md#ah-r1274) is Carlo’s stepsibling. Carlo makes room at the table. Walter brings whatever was actually growing or affordable that week. The meal sometimes produces a solution. Even when it does not, everyone goes away knowing what the other household is up against. Asked about *The other side of the cove*, Walter says, “I want my mother's work named with her proper job title.”

**People in this story.** [Elsa Allen](#ah-r0696). The seller sets a little spice packet aside for a customer fasting until night.

**Personality.** Carlo takes a rejection personally for a day and analytically on the second morning. Carlo can be generous with a rival once the first night's sleep has intervened.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a pale blue work shirt and one cuff folded back twice. Carlo has a deep crease beside the mouth when smiling, with short black hair swept back. Carlo squares up pieces of paper while thinking during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- A customer wants a bun saved until evening. Carlo writes the customer's name and collection time on the wrapped bun.
- An envelope of wages is found under a café chair. Carlo finds a second person to witness its return so an honest mistake won't cost a worker their pay.
- If someone else wins the opportunity, Carlo turns up the next morning with the one piece of advice that would have helped either of them.

**Appearance.** Terry has a deep crease beside the mouth when smiling. Look for dark brown curls cut close behind the ears. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a pale blue work shirt with a folded shopping list secured by a miniature clip. In conversation Terry draws small circles on a tabletop while waiting.

**Under pressure**

## Layla Jimenez

AH-R0694 · Age 58 · Former kitchen lead · Home — the streets around Foundry Lofts

**Life in the city.** “I still remember tasting everything in the shop before a fever took my sense of smell.” On Tuesday, near the corner outside Coastal Barber, Layla usually checks the board before the regulars give a more colorful account of the news.

**In their own words.** “I lost my sense of smell in a fever. I recognized everybody's faces from last winter's batch.”

**What Layla wants.** To recognize a fresh batch by another sense.

**Family.** [Tom Moreno](../07/13-a-dance-for-two-fathers.md#ah-r1022) is Layla’s stepsibling. Layla picks the meeting place. Tom sets the time to leave. It lets them stay close without turning a family visit into the loss of an entire day. They have both had stretches when that distinction mattered. Asked about *A dance for two fathers*, Tom says, “I raised her. The song she picked was the one her birth father used to play for her and I know every word.”

**People in this story.** [Keiko Ruiz](#ah-r0700). A cook who can't smell can still follow a holiday gift to the right neighbor.

**Personality.** Layla has a strong competitive streak and a very soft spot for the person finishing last. A prize tastes better when everybody had enough time to enter.

**Appearance.** Layla has dark brown curls cut close behind the ears, heavy brows and an unusually delicate jaw, and a favorite piece of clothing, a black overshirt with pearl buttons. It usually goes with a fabric zip pull cut from a school scarf. When somebody else has the floor, Layla takes off the watch before a long conversation.

**Under pressure**

- A customer wants a bun saved until evening. Layla writes the customer's name and collection time on the wrapped bun.
- Someone offers a cash reward for naming a rumored culprit. Layla asks what evidence supports the accusation and declines to supply a name on gossip alone.
- If accused of favoritism, Layla explains exactly what help was offered and offers the same practical help to the accuser.

## Devin Ames

AH-R0697 · Age 36 · Spice delivery driver · Home — Eastside

**Life in the city.** “I still remember spilling red pepper all over the warehouse stairs.” On Thursday, near the foot of Harbor Viaduct, Devin pauses where the machinery overhead makes two people stand close enough to hear each other.

**In their own words.** “I brought the wrong sack and almost lost the account. The apprentices wanted a repeat of the mistake.”

**What Devin wants.** To keep the account after the supplier's mistake.

**Family.** [Toby Hsu](../09/14-the-missing-sunday.md#ah-r1335) is Devin’s first cousin once removed. Devin leaves the first voice message. Toby calls back when there is finally a little privacy. In their family a missed ring can mean a bus, a customer, a sleeping child or a bad hour. Neither treats it as an answer in itself. Asked about *The missing Sunday*, Toby says, “I live next door and didn't know he followed our team.”

**People in this story.** [Nina Fraser](#ah-r0699). The wrongly delivered sack still carries the aunt's recognizably old packing mark. Also in the story is [Seth Roberts](#ah-r0691) (baker).

**Personality.** Devin listens for the familiar version of a story and then for the one detail that changes it. Friends bring problems here when they want to be heard twice.

**Appearance.** Devin has auburn hair cut square at the nape, a long face and high cheekbones, and a favorite piece of clothing, a cream pullover. It usually goes with a folded shopping list secured by a miniature clip. When somebody else has the floor, Devin takes off the watch before a long conversation.

**Under pressure**

- The spice buyer wants the mistaken sack returned. Devin sets aside a sample and confirms which holiday orders depend on it.
- Someone begins blaming the youngest worker for a missing item. Devin brings the worker into the conversation and asks what they remember without feeding them a story.
- When a second witness disagrees, Devin keeps the accounts separate until the witnesses have had a chance to meet.

## Keiko Ruiz

AH-R0700 · Age 67 · Recipe keeper and caterer · Home — the western blocks near Ferry Street Rooms

**Life in the city.** “I still remember taking a holiday cake to a doorstep with no wreath.” People around the courtyard by Ferry Street Rooms know Keiko by a small Wednesday habit. Keiko prefers the bench where the sound of the shop door reaches before the visitor does.

**In their own words.** “I have the recipe nobody else remembers. It calls for leaving one bun on a lonely neighbor's step.”

**What Keiko wants.** To leave a warm bun on a lonely doorstep.

**Family.** [Grey Good](../02/09-a-wall-of-birthdays.md#ah-r0232) is Keiko’s half-sibling. Keiko chooses whether they walk home or sit a while. Grey brings a spare shopping bag. That small arrangement gives both a reason to see each other on weeks when asking for company outright feels difficult. Asked about *A wall of birthdays*, Grey says, “I papered over the wall because my boy never reached his next mark.”

**People in this story.** [Layla Jimenez](#ah-r0694). A cook who can't smell can still follow a holiday gift to the right neighbor.

**Personality.** Keiko never tires of being shown how somebody makes a living. Keiko can ask an expert question of a craftsperson and still mishandle a simple emotional cue from a friend.

**Appearance.** Keiko has two white streaks in otherwise dark hair, a broad nose and a deep dimple on one cheek, and a favorite piece of clothing, a narrow-striped sweater. It usually goes with a narrow brass watch set a little fast. When somebody else has the floor, Keiko starts to lift a hand, then waits to be invited to speak.

**Under pressure**

- A customer wants a bun saved until evening. Keiko writes the customer's name and collection time on the wrapped bun.
- A coworker arrives late after a night at the hospital. Keiko asks them privately what can be shared about the delay and respects the answer.
- When a relative hints at needing company, Keiko may initially offer a repair, but sits down as soon as the request becomes clear.
