## Jo Grant

AH-R0931 · Age 45 · Police communications dispatcher · Home — Coast Ridge · Department ID ah-pd-113 · Communications, watch 1, badge 113

**Life in the city.** “I still remember typing a caption from the notes in a wet coat.” People around the brick passage off the shop row know Jo by a small Sunday habit. Jo takes the longer way when the smell from the ovens reaches the pavement.

**In their own words.** “I wrote the caption from a shorthand note. I remember the shopkeeper bringing tea to the waiting families.”

**What Jo wants.** To replace the inaccurate caption under personal responsibility.

**Family.** [Orlando Dinh](../09/10-a-four-o-clock-kitchen.md#ah-r1292) is Jo’s second cousin. Jo brings the paper and a pen. Orlando decides what stays off the paper. They have helped each other make decisions that were far easier to discuss once the figures could be moved around. The decisions themselves still belong to the person asking. Asked about *A four o'clock kitchen*, Orlando says, “I work the port shift and the first warm meal makes me less short-tempered with my kid after work.”

**People in this story.** [Luis Soto](#ah-r0938) (department colleague). A caption writer and the colleague who stayed silent both have something specific to correct.

**Personality.** Jo reads little signals of discomfort very accurately and has an unfortunate habit of overruling people for their own good. The friends who say stop are the ones Jo trusts most.

**Appearance.** Jo has black hair twisted into a small knot, fine brows over wide-set eyes, and a favorite piece of clothing, a mustard canvas overshirt. It usually goes with a pair of reading glasses in a cloth sleeve. When somebody else has the floor, Jo steadies a cup with both hands while listening.

**Under pressure**

- A newspaper repeats the old display caption. Jo gets the shopkeeper's preferred wording to the editor before passing the story on.
- A neighbor offers to pay for a favor that was meant kindly. Jo accepts a small agreed amount if paying lets the neighbor keep their pride.
- If told to back off, Jo does so immediately and stays available at a distance the other person chooses.

## Ada Flynn

AH-R0932 · Age 37 · Forensic analyst · Home — Midtown · Department ID ah-pd-114 · Forensics, watch 1, badge 114

**Life in the city.** “I still remember my son repeating a story I'd only half checked.” People around Juniper Street know Ada by a small Saturday habit. Ada knows which of the west-side porches has a seat in the shade.

**In their own words.** “I read the wrong name to my son for years. I want him beside me when the right one goes up.”

**What Ada wants.** To show the corrected name to the son.

**Family.** [Damian Neal](../03/07-overdue-by-a-lifetime.md#ah-r0366) is Ada’s half-sibling. Ada calls back when there is finally a little privacy. Damian leaves the first voice message. In their family a missed ring can mean a bus, a customer, a sleeping child or a bad hour. Neither treats it as an answer in itself. Asked about *Overdue by a lifetime*, Damian says, “I wrote my crush's name under the flap. They're coming to the reading with their spouse.”

**People in this story.** [Ari Lane](#ah-r0939). The two grown children in the story deserve to know what name will go up this time.

**Personality.** Ada has a quick wit and an equally quick sense of shame. A joke is often Ada's way of finding out whether a room is friendly enough for a serious question.

**Appearance.** Ada has a crooked front tooth visible when laughing. Look for a long plait tucked inside the collar. Off duty, the familiar outfit is a black overshirt with pearl buttons with a folded shopping list secured by a miniature clip. In conversation Ada turns fully toward the person speaking.

**Under pressure**

- The shopkeeper asks for a public correction. Ada agrees a place and size for the correction with the person who was named.
- A street musician can't finish a familiar song. Ada starts a quiet conversation with the most impatient onlookers so the musician can recover their own time.
- If the joke lands badly, Ada seeks out the person it hurt before telling anybody why it was supposed to be funny.

## Nolan Reed

**Appearance.** Nolan is easy to recognize by short sandy hair and a marked widow's peak and a strong profile and a little space between the front teeth. Usually dressed in a brown corduroy jacket with one loose pocket corner secured with green thread, Nolan looks down for a moment before saying something difficult when listening.

**Under pressure**
