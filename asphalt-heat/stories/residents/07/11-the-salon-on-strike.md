# The salon on strike

[The families who come back](README.md) · [City directory](../directory.md)

**Verve Nail Studio.** Two salon workers refuse an unpaid promotional evening. Their colleagues disagree about what a regular owes the people who do the work.

[Karim Byrd](#ah-r1001) · [Aldo Dixon](#ah-r1002) · [Isla Joseph](#ah-r1003) · [Chris Moss](#ah-r1004) · [Leah Smith](#ah-r1005) · [Milo Roche](#ah-r1006) · [Sadie Carver](#ah-r1007) · [Hollis Aranda](#ah-r1008) · [Evelyn Wade](#ah-r1009) · [Tali Harris](#ah-r1010)

## Karim Byrd

AH-R1001 · Age 31 · Salon freelancer · Home — the neighborhood around Market Flats

**Life in the city.** “I still remember agreeing to a display in return for a better chair.” People around Tideline Market know Karim by a small Saturday habit. Karim picks out the bruised fruit first and asks the seller what will ripen by supper.

**In their own words.** “I volunteered because I need new clients. I forgot my coworkers need the hours paid today.”

**What Karim wants.** To gain new business without obliging coworkers to work free.

**Family.** [Tori Gran](../01/14-two-portions.md#ah-r0135) is Karim’s cousin. Karim chooses the music. Tori puts the kettle on. The cups come out before either brings up the reason for the visit. They trust a difficult conversation more if it has had an ordinary beginning. Asked about *Two portions*, Tori says, “Our shop donated tins nobody likes. I was embarrassed hearing what the cook did to make them edible.”

**People in this story.** [Isla Joseph](#ah-r1003). A worker hoping to gain clients speaks directly with the person who booked the unpaid promotion.

**Personality.** Karim has a talent for getting adversaries to laugh at exactly the same thing. Karim knows better than to expect the laugh to settle what they are arguing about.

**Appearance.** Karim has a heart-shaped face and prominent ears. Look for short black hair swept back. After the last appointment, the familiar outfit is a rust-colored canvas coat with a pocket notebook shaped by years of sitting on it. In conversation Karim grins on one side while the eyes stay serious.

**Under pressure**

- A promotional client insists on keeping the booking. Karim asks who will be paid to staff the booking before promising it can go ahead.
- A power cut leaves two neighbors waiting on the stairs. Karim asks which apartment is expecting someone home and makes sure that household knows about the delay.
- When people declare an argument over too soon, Karim asks them to describe one concrete thing that will be different tomorrow.

## Aldo Dixon

AH-R1002 · Age 39 · Salon employee · Home — Old Town

**Life in the city.** “I still remember walking away from a salon with glitter still under my nails.” Sunday often includes a stop at the crossing outside Verve Nail Studio, where Aldo notices whose chair is empty and who has stayed talking after an appointment.

**In their own words.** “I quit my last salon over this and swore the next owner would hear my reasons before getting my notice.”

**What Aldo wants.** To get an answer from this owner before resigning.

**Family.** [Terry Lin](../06/03-money-after-marriage.md#ah-r0778) is Aldo’s stepsibling. Aldo chooses whether they walk home or sit a while. Terry brings a spare shopping bag. That small arrangement gives both a reason to see each other on weeks when asking for company outright feels difficult. Asked about *Money after marriage*, Terry says, “I used to get invited to both houses. Once I put a price on the company neither was sure I could still come to dinner.”

**People in this story.** [Tali Harris](#ah-r1010) (salon owner). The worker who would quit explains the exact terms the owner once protested herself.

**Personality.** Aldo enjoys bargaining, including over things already settled. It is partly a joke and partly a test of whether the other person feels able to say no.

**Appearance.** After the last appointment, look for a moss-green cardigan and a square of clean cloth for polishing smudges. Aldo has a broad nose and a deep dimple on one cheek, with short black hair swept back. Aldo folds a paper napkin into quarters without noticing during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- An apprentice asks if declining will cost future shifts. Aldo asks the owner to give the apprentice a direct answer in front of a witness of their choosing.
- An envelope of wages is found under a café chair. Aldo asks the cashier to hold it and lets a claimant identify the writing and amount privately.
- When a friend starts agreeing too quickly, Aldo puts the deal away and suggests a walk.

## Isla Joseph

AH-R1003 · Age 46 · Events planner · Home — Midtown

**Life in the city.** “I still remember planning a reception nobody thought to pay the cleaners for.” On Friday, near the blocks below Foundry Lofts, Isla checks the little hand-lettered notices around Harbour Supply.

**In their own words.** “I booked the party and invited my millionaire boss. I can cancel my own celebration without canceling everybody's wages.”

**What Isla wants.** To cancel the private celebration without canceling wages.

**Family.** [Anna Bishop](../05/05-the-clean-ledger.md#ah-r0641) is Isla’s older cousin. Isla is free to hang up first. Anna makes the call. Some of their best calls last less than a minute. A change of tone is sometimes all the news either one needs. Asked about *The clean ledger*, Anna says, “I want my niece's graduation dress back. A price in a notebook won't fit her.”

**People in this story.** [Karim Byrd](#ah-r1001). A worker hoping to gain clients speaks directly with the person who booked the unpaid promotion.

**Personality.** Isla has a long memory for unfair prices and a short one for small insults. Isla can get along perfectly well with an enemy provided the account between them is clear.

**Appearance.** Isla has close-cropped dark curls, heavy brows and an unusually delicate jaw, and a favorite piece of clothing, a washed violet jersey. It usually goes with one loose pocket corner secured with green thread. When somebody else has the floor, Isla listens with the head tilted a little to the left.

**Under pressure**

- A promotional client insists on keeping the booking. Isla asks who will be paid to staff the booking before promising it can go ahead.
- A cyclist goes down in a crowded crossing. Isla stays with the rider after the people who saw the fall start drifting away.
- If a friend borrows money, Isla agrees on an amount both can bear losing before any cash changes hands.

## Chris Moss

AH-R1004 · Age 35 · Beauty supplier · Home — the western blocks near Ferry Street Rooms

**Life in the city.** “I still remember a bottle promotion that sold out while the trainees did all the washing up.” On Thursday, near Studio Boulevard, Chris waits by the window where the whole crossing can be seen without leaning into the road.

**In their own words.** “I make commission on the bottles. I brought my sales figures so the assistants can point to what their work earns.”

**What Chris wants.** To show what the assistants' labor earned.

**Family.** [Kai Mercer](../09/07-school-after-sixty.md#ah-r1267) is Chris’s second cousin. Chris keeps the photographs. Kai knows which ones can be shared. They enjoy remembering people in public and also know what it feels like to have a private afternoon turned into an anecdote for strangers. Asked about *School after sixty*, Kai says, “I can translate the questions but not the answer my aunt wants to give.”

**People in this story.** [Evelyn Wade](#ah-r1009). Sales figures give the worker financing a colleague's absence a better bargaining position.

**Personality.** Chris takes a rejection personally for a day and analytically on the second morning. Chris can be generous with a rival once the first night's sleep has intervened.

**Appearance.** Chris is easy to recognize by a dark undercut with a longer wave on top and a wide brow and a narrow jaw. Usually dressed in a boxy denim jacket with a printed handkerchief folded into the pocket, Chris listens with the head tilted a little to the left when listening.

**Under pressure**

- An apprentice asks if declining will cost future shifts. Chris asks the owner to give the apprentice a direct answer in front of a witness of their choosing.
- An unfamiliar person asks for directions but cannot read the street signs. Chris finds out what the destination actually looks like to the traveler and gives directions in those terms.
- If someone else wins the opportunity, Chris turns up the next morning with the one piece of advice that would have helped either of them.

## Leah Smith

Some of their best calls last less than a minute. A change of tone is sometimes all the news either one needs. **Appearance.** Leah has a scattering of freckles across the bridge of the nose. Look for close-cropped dark curls. Away from the heat of the kitchen, the familiar outfit is a gray fisher's sweater with a copper-colored clasp that has been repaired by hand. In conversation Leah draws breath as if to speak and lets someone else go first.

**Under pressure**

- When a beginner is embarrassed, Leah describes a first attempt of their own in enough detail to make the comparison fair.

## Milo Roche

AH-R1006 · Age 51 · Coffee shop owner · Home — the streets around Foundry Lofts

**Life in the city.** “I still remember a folding chair used for my first staff hearing.” Friday often includes a stop at the market by Market Flats, where Milo often waits until the delivery baskets are stacked before going in.

**In their own words.** “I rent the space next door. I offered enough folding chairs for the meeting, whatever they decide.”

**What Milo wants.** To host a meeting where either choice is welcome.

**Family.** [Lars James](../08/02-land-without-cranes.md#ah-r1061) is Milo’s first cousin once removed. Milo leaves the first voice message. Lars calls back when there is finally a little privacy. In their family a missed ring can mean a bus, a customer, a sleeping child or a bad hour. Neither treats it as an answer in itself. Asked about *Land without cranes*, Lars says, “I want a desk job here someday, but the new rents won't buy one for anyone I know.”

**People in this story.** [Hollis Aranda](#ah-r1008). A meeting next door gives the advertiser a real public update on the canceled event.

**Personality.** Milo has a strong competitive streak and a very soft spot for the person finishing last. A prize tastes better when everybody had enough time to enter.

**Appearance.** Milo has short, uneven waves that refuse to lie flat, fine brows over wide-set eyes, and a favorite piece of clothing, a soft blue checked shirt. It usually goes with a pressed flower kept in the clear back of a phone case. When somebody else has the floor, Milo rubs a thumb along the edge of the nearest pocket.

**Under pressure**

- An apprentice asks if declining will cost future shifts. Milo asks the owner to give the apprentice a direct answer in front of a witness of their choosing.
- Someone offers a cash reward for naming a rumored culprit. Milo asks what evidence supports the accusation and declines to supply a name on gossip alone.
- If accused of favoritism, Milo explains exactly what help was offered and offers the same practical help to the accuser.

## Evelyn Wade

**Family.** [Dario Allard](../03/04-the-spare-spectacles.md#ah-r0336) is Evelyn’s younger cousin. Their family had a rule about visiting someone who had fallen on hard times. Bring enough food for the next day and bring a reason for an ordinary conversation. Evelyn can rustle up a convincing practical excuse for a visit. Asked about *The spare spectacles*, Dario says, “I keep my dad's spare pair in my bag to see menus the way he did.”

**People in this story.** [Chris Moss](#ah-r1004). Sales figures give the worker financing a colleague's absence a better bargaining position.

**Personality.** Evelyn listens for the familiar version of a story and then for the one detail that changes it. Friends bring problems here when they want to be heard twice.

**Appearance.** Evelyn is easy to recognize by short black hair swept back and one cheek that creases before the other. Usually dressed in a faded indigo overshirt with a pocket notebook shaped by years of sitting on it, Evelyn looks at people's hands before looking at what they carry when listening.

**Under pressure**

- A promotional client insists on keeping the booking. Evelyn asks who will be paid to staff the booking before promising it can go ahead.
- Someone begins blaming the youngest worker for a missing item. Evelyn brings the worker into the conversation and asks what they remember without feeding them a story.
- When a second witness disagrees, Evelyn keeps the accounts separate until the witnesses have had a chance to meet.

## Tali Harris

AH-R1010 · Age 55 · Salon owner and nail artist · Home — Old Town

**Life in the city.** “I still remember working a full evening with pay promised in samples.” Friday often includes a stop at the bank steps, where Tali takes a few minutes to read the public notices, especially the ones written in a hurry.

**In their own words.** “I own the salon. I used to complain about my own boss doing exactly this, which the oldest technician remembers.”

**What Tali wants.** To get the offer into terms the old shop self would have accepted.

**Family.** [Ana Crane](../08/13-the-understudy-sings.md#ah-r1179) is Tali’s second cousin. Tali remembers the birthdays. Ana asks what the birthday guest would like this year. A present that was perfect at sixteen may be completely wrong at forty. They take the trouble of finding out. Asked about *The understudy sings*, Ana says, “I am the parent's friend who wanted a benefit concert.”

**People in this story.** [Aldo Dixon](#ah-r1002) (salon worker). The worker who would quit explains the exact terms the owner once protested herself.

**Personality.** Tali talks very little about private fear and recognizes it quickly in strangers. Offering a choice of seats is one of Tali's ways of saying that it is all right to leave.

**Appearance.** Tali is easy to recognize by a smooth side part with one rebellious curl and a wide brow and a narrow jaw. Usually dressed in a long navy raincoat with a folded shopping list secured by a miniature clip, Tali draws breath as if to speak and lets someone else go first when listening.

**Under pressure**

- An apprentice asks if declining will cost future shifts. Tali asks the owner to give the apprentice a direct answer in front of a witness of their choosing.
- A power cut leaves two neighbors waiting on the stairs. Tali asks which apartment is expecting someone home and makes sure that household knows about the delay.
- When pressed to explain someone else's silence, Tali says only what happened and lets the absent person keep their reasons.
