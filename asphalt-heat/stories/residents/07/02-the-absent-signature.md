**Family.** [Lucinda Duran](../02/13-the-quiet-verse.md#ah-r0276) is Sage’s cousin. Sage chooses whether they walk home or sit a while. Lucinda brings a spare shopping bag. **People in this story.** [Mara Bly](#ah-r0917). They agree to ask the missing aunt before giving her address to people who want a signature.

**Personality.** Sage enjoys routines and will defend the right of someone else to break one. As a result, Sage is often everyone's favorite cover for a missed morning.

**Appearance.** Sage has thick, tightly coiled hair, a strong profile and a little space between the front teeth, and a favorite piece of clothing, a plum-colored cardigan. It usually goes with a folded shopping list secured by a miniature clip. When somebody else has the floor, Sage touches a knuckle to the chin when searching for a word.

**Under pressure**

- A relative wants the absent signer's address. Sage asks the absent person what address, if any, can be shared.
- A friend asks for a loan due back before payday. Sage offers to cover one concrete expense and leaves the friend room to say the expense isn't the real problem.
- If a family plan changes at the door, Sage keeps a place for the late person without making the others postpone their meal.
