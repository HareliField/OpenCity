## Kathleen Roy

AH-R0901 · Age 44 · School uniform presser · Home — the western blocks near Ferry Street Rooms

**Life in the city.** “I still remember saving for a blazer in a biscuit jar.” On Thursday, near Breakwater Way, Kathleen stops on the landward side of the cycle track to watch the first boats turn for home.

**In their own words.** “I earned the uniform money washing hotel sheets. I sat down on the stairs before I could tell my boy.”

**What Kathleen wants.** To restore a dry set of school clothes by morning.

**Family.** [Hamish Mann](../09/05-a-clean-coat-for-court.md#ah-r1249) is Kathleen’s cousin. Kathleen offers the first invitation. Hamish gets to change the plan. They learned that a promise to visit is easier to keep when changing a time does not count as letting down the whole family. Asked about *A clean coat for court*, Hamish says, “I had planned to wait outside. Seeing the lender sit alone made me move one chair closer.”

**People in this story.** [Lily Parra](#ah-r0902). A damaged shirt and a borrowed blazer form one usable first-day uniform.

**Personality.** Kathleen is kind in very practical ways and prickly about being thanked in public. Kathleen loves being recognized by the people who notice what happens after the guests have left.

**Appearance.** Kathleen is easy to recognize by a smooth side part with one rebellious curl and a broad nose and a deep dimple on one cheek. Usually dressed in a dark green knitted vest with a thin silver ring worn on a cord, Kathleen presses the lips together to keep a smile in when listening.

**Under pressure**

- A student turns up in the wrong uniform. Kathleen gets the child through the gate before discussing the uniform with an adult.
- A friend asks for a loan due back before payday. Kathleen agrees on a sum and a date with the friend in private, keeping the arrangement out of family gossip.
- If somebody calls for applause, Kathleen gives the credit to a specific helper and slips out before the photograph.

## Lily Parra

AH-R0902 · Age 36 · Secondhand uniform sorter · Home — the blocks north of the port

**People in this story.** [Kathleen Roy](#ah-r0901). A damaged shirt and a borrowed blazer form one usable first-day uniform.

**Appearance.** Lily has a broad nose and a deep dimple on one cheek. Look for a center part and straight chestnut hair. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a brown corduroy jacket with a thin silver ring worn on a cord. In conversation Lily takes off the watch before a long conversation.

**Under pressure**

- An argument on the pavement makes a bystander feel unsafe. Lily stays beside the bystander in a public place and reports only what happened in sight and earshot.
- When a relative dismisses a little luxury, Lily asks which ordinary pleasure the relative would give up first.

## Terry Morse

AH-R0903 · Age 49 · Parent and lift attendant · Home — the western blocks near Ferry Street Rooms

**Life in the city.** “I still remember a teacher mistaking a faded skirt for the wrong color.” On Saturday, near the brick passage off the shop row, Terry takes the longer way when the smell from the ovens reaches the pavement.

**In their own words.** “I thought my daughter was being vain about the right shade of skirt until I remembered my first day here.”

**What Terry wants.** To let the daughter choose which uniform fixes matter.

**Family.** [Drew Milo](../01/10-the-thirteenth-doughnut.md#ah-r0093) is Terry’s second cousin. Terry brings something that can still be enjoyed tomorrow. Drew checks the date twice. Arrangements do change in their family. Taking a delay seriously without treating it as an insult took both of them years. Asked about *The thirteenth doughnut*, Drew says, “I got the first free one and ate it sitting on the loading step.”

**People in this story.** [Dale Valdez](#ah-r0910). A concerned parent can meet a teacher wearing the same ordinary clothes as a pupil.

**Personality.** Terry can be wonderfully decisive about an emergency and maddeningly undecided about a gift. This is because a person can say precisely what hurts and rarely says exactly what they want.

**Appearance.** Terry has a heart-shaped face and prominent ears. Look for short, uneven waves that refuse to lie flat. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a faded indigo overshirt with one loose pocket corner secured with green thread. In conversation Terry raises one eyebrow long before saying yes.

**Under pressure**

- A student turns up in the wrong uniform. Terry gets the child through the gate before discussing the uniform with an adult.
- A customer is short at the till while a queue forms. Terry asks the cashier quietly which item can be set aside without making the customer explain their finances to the line.
- If a neighbor offers a costly present, Terry asks for an afternoon to work out whether accepting it would put someone else in debt.

## Keir Pierce

AH-R0904 · Age 32 · Laundry mechanic · Home — the western residential streets

**Life in the city.** “I still remember my own uniform left to dry over the shop till.” Monday often includes a stop at Maré Coffee, where Keir likes the table where people coming off shift stop checking the time.

**In their own words.** “I caused the leak moving a hose to mop the floor. I reopened the shop on my evening off.”

**What Keir wants.** To cover an evening wash shift to undo the damage.

**Family.** [Lena Osborne](../03/09-the-upstairs-piano.md#ah-r0381) is Keir’s half-sibling. Keir chooses whether they walk home or sit a while. Lena brings a spare shopping bag. That small arrangement gives both a reason to see each other on weeks when asking for company outright feels difficult. Asked about *The upstairs piano*, Lena says, “I bought it so my son would come home to practice.”

**People in this story.** [Rae Vargas](#ah-r0909). The repair shift is ordered by the bus schedule the pupils worked out for the iron. Also in the story is [Dale Valdez](#ah-r0910) (school contact).

**Personality.** Keir can be lavish with time and quite careful with cash. A free afternoon is sometimes the biggest gift Keir can afford to give, and family knows to take it seriously.

**Appearance.** Keir is easy to recognize by thick, tightly coiled hair and two dimples that appear only with a full smile. Usually dressed in a cropped bottle-green coat with a knotted piece of sail thread on a key ring, Keir looks down for a moment before saying something difficult when listening.

**Under pressure**

- A donor asks to photograph the children receiving clothes. Keir asks the children and families whether they want to appear in the picture.
- An envelope of wages is found under a café chair. Keir brings the envelope to the counter unopened and stays long enough to make a note of who accepted it.
- If invited to an expensive outing, Keir suggests meeting afterward and explains why without asking the others to abandon their plans.

**Under pressure**

## Dale Valdez

AH-R0910 · Age 48 · Primary teacher · Home — the western blocks near Ferry Street Rooms

**Life in the city.** “I still remember borrowing a sports jersey on my first day of work.” People around the corner outside Coastal Barber know Dale by a small Tuesday habit. Dale usually checks the board before the regulars give a more colorful account of the news.

**In their own words.** “I am the teacher at the gate. I asked the head to join me in ordinary clothes on Monday morning.”

**What Dale wants.** To get the adults at the gate into ordinary clothes.

**Family.** [Emil Palmer](../08/08-a-summer-marriage.md#ah-r1129) is Dale’s younger cousin. One family member never got a driver's license and learned the city by the feel of its bus stops. After dark the person with the smallest salary was often the one who knew the safest ride home. Dale can give directions in terms of places to rest. Asked about *A summer marriage*, Emil says, “I donated a shift to the bride. She returned it because knowing the children had a lifeguard mattered more.”

**People in this story.** [Terry Morse](#ah-r0903). A concerned parent can meet a teacher wearing the same ordinary clothes as a pupil. Also in the story is [Keir Pierce](#ah-r0904) (uniform washer).

**Personality.** Dale is fond of ambitious ideas and deeply suspicious of grand openings. The question Dale asks at any launch is who has the keys for cleaning up a month later.

**Appearance.** Dale has a dark undercut with a longer wave on top, a long face and high cheekbones, and a favorite piece of clothing, a mustard canvas overshirt. It usually goes with a comb in a sleeve sewn into the lining. When somebody else has the floor, Dale starts to lift a hand, then waits to be invited to speak.

**Under pressure**

- A donor asks to photograph the children receiving clothes. Dale asks the children and families whether they want to appear in the picture.
- Someone offers a cash reward for naming a rumored culprit. Dale asks what evidence supports the accusation and declines to supply a name on gossip alone.
- If offered a ceremonial role, Dale accepts only after finding out whether the person doing the regular work can come too.
