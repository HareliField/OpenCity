## Gareth Roth

AH-R0301 · Age 39 · Courier · Home — the blocks north of the port

**Life in the city.** “I still remember my father's tunes coming through his workshop wall.” On Sunday, near the market by Market Flats, Gareth often waits until the delivery baskets are stacked before going in.

**In their own words.** “My father whistled that song through our last argument. I need its second line.”

**What Gareth wants.** To recover the tune's missing verse.

**Family.** [Carmen Saye](../01/12-the-last-green.md#ah-r0118) is Gareth’s cousin. Gareth brings whatever was actually growing or affordable that week. Carmen makes room at the table. The meal sometimes produces a solution. Even when it does not, everyone goes away knowing what the other household is up against. Asked about *The last green*, Carmen says, “I used to take his cab after my double shifts.”

**People in this story.** [Alejandro Sanders](#ah-r0302). They compare a familiar song to the actual pitches of the three machines.

**Personality.** Gareth enjoys routines and will defend the right of someone else to break one. As a result, Gareth is often everyone's favorite cover for a missed morning.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a faded indigo overshirt and a pressed flower kept in the clear back of a phone case. Gareth has a heart-shaped face and prominent ears, with a long plait tucked inside the collar. Gareth rubs a thumb along the edge of the nearest pocket during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- The washer bearing goes quiet mid-song. Gareth listens for the missing note and lets maintenance know what changed.
- An argument on the pavement makes a bystander feel unsafe. Gareth puts distance between the frightened person and the argument and asks nearby staff for help.
- If a family plan changes at the door, Gareth keeps a place for the late person without making the others postpone their meal.

## Alejandro Sanders

AH-R0302 · Age 51 · Piano tuner · Home — Eastside

**Life in the city.** “I still remember a student tipping me in bruised peaches.” On Sunday, near the courtyard by Ferry Street Rooms, Alejandro prefers the bench where the sound of the shop door reaches before the visitor does.

**In their own words.** “I have tuned pianos for years. This is the first chord I've been paid to leave out of tune.”

**What Alejandro wants.** To record the imperfect chord before repairs.

**Family.** [Alvin Chapman](../02/11-one-sleeve-short.md#ah-r0260) is Alejandro’s half-sibling. Alejandro is free to hang up first. Alvin makes the call. Some of their best calls last less than a minute. A change of tone is sometimes all the news either one needs. Asked about *One sleeve short*, Alvin says, “I hired him to meet customers. On day one he showed me how much you can learn from an unfinished sleeve.”

**People in this story.** [Gareth Roth](#ah-r0301). They compare a familiar song to the actual pitches of the three machines. Also in the story is [Franklin Brandt](#ah-r0309) (noise complainant).

**Personality.** Alejandro is cheerful on a bad morning and unexpectedly melancholy after a success. Finishing a hard thing means losing the daily company of the people who worked on it.

**Appearance.** Alejandro has fine brows over wide-set eyes. Look for a center part and straight chestnut hair. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a washed violet jersey with a cloth patch so neatly matched it only shows in strong light. In conversation Alejandro grins on one side while the eyes stay serious.

**Under pressure**

- A tired neighbor asks the singers to move. Alejandro asks when the neighbor sleeps and helps arrange a quieter hour.
- A friend asks for a loan due back before payday. Alejandro offers to cover one concrete expense and leaves the friend room to say the expense isn't the real problem.
- When everyone else wants to celebrate immediately, Alejandro helps set the table, then takes a short turn outside alone.

## Thomas Silva

AH-R0303 · Age 34 · Bakery night worker · Home — the neighborhood around Market Flats

**Life in the city.** “I still remember rocking a borrowed pram with my ankle.” Wednesday often includes a stop at the downtown fountain, where Thomas keeps a little time free to hear who is playing at the far end of the park.

**In their own words.** “I hear the dryers through my pillow. I want one morning when the neighbors sing with them.”

**What Thomas wants.** To agree one undisturbed morning with the singers.

**Family.** [Quinn Laurent](../07/15-the-day-sailor-s-divorce.md#ah-r1047) is Thomas’s older cousin. Thomas calls about good news first. Quinn is allowed to ask about the bad news afterward. Neither wants every family conversation to begin with a bill, a diagnosis or an apology. Asked about *The day sailor's divorce*, Quinn says, “I kept hearing who paid most. I brought the maintenance log with everyone's cold hands in it.”

**People in this story.** [Franklin Brandt](#ah-r0309). The people trying to sleep get a say in when the morning choir rehearses.

**Personality.** Thomas finds names easy and faces hard on a crowded street. People who assume they have been snubbed usually get a warmer greeting once they speak.

**Appearance.** Away from the heat of the kitchen, look for a red windbreaker and a cloth patch so neatly matched it only shows in strong light. Thomas has full cheeks and a mouth that pulls slightly to one side, with brown hair pinned away from the temples. Thomas touches a knuckle to the chin when searching for a word during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- The washer bearing goes quiet mid-song. Thomas listens for the missing note and lets maintenance know what changed.
- A street musician can't finish a familiar song. Thomas keeps listening through the pause and lets the musician choose the next note.
- When somebody waves from across traffic, Thomas waits at the corner to hear their voice and give them a proper answer.

## Russell Hooper

**Life in the city.** “I still remember my baby's first nap through the bakery alarm.” Wednesday often includes a stop at the steps at Pier Beach, where Russell watches the working part of the waterfront before turning toward the open water.

**In their own words.** “My baby sleeps only during the spin cycle. The regulars saved the gentlest machine for us.”

**What Russell wants.** To save a gentle cycle for the baby's naps.

**Family.** [Nolan Costa](../02/14-saturday-keys.md#ah-r0281) is Russell’s older relative by marriage. Russell is allowed to ask about the bad news afterward. Nolan calls about good news first. Neither wants every family conversation to begin with a bill, a diagnosis or an apology. Asked about *Saturday keys*, Nolan says, “I memorized which doors stick because I couldn't read the old tags.”

**People in this story.** [Irene Baptiste](#ah-r0305). A useful lullaby depends on choosing the right machine and the right load.

**Personality.** Is a careful borrower and a reckless lender. Things leave this person's house with elaborate instructions and very little expectation of ever coming back.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a black overshirt with pearl buttons and a narrow brass watch set a little fast. Russell has a flat bridge to the nose and a mobile brow, with a soft fringe that falls across one eyebrow. Russell looks toward the door whenever a name is called during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- A tired neighbor asks the singers to move. Russell asks when the neighbor sleeps and helps arrange a quieter hour.
- A colleague says somebody used the wrong name in public. Russell offers to help correct the printed name and leaves it to the colleague whether any public explanation is wanted.
- If a stranger returns something damaged, Russell listens first to the account of how it was actually used.

## Irene Baptiste

AH-R0305 · Age 23 · Kitchen hand · Home — Eastside

**Life in the city.** “I still remember washing a restaurant apron until it turned pink.” On Wednesday, near Breakwater Way, Irene stops on the landward side of the cycle track to watch the first boats turn for home.

**In their own words.** “I put three different loads in to test my theory and dyed my best shirt.”

**What Irene wants.** To replace the shirt without blaming the washers.

**Family.** [Mia Nair](../05/05-the-clean-ledger.md#ah-r0650) is Irene’s first cousin once removed. Irene offers the first invitation. Mia gets to change the plan. They learned that a promise to visit is easier to keep when changing a time does not count as letting down the whole family. Asked about *The clean ledger*, Mia says, “I want to be the last name he reads. I owe him an apology he never put in his ledger.”

**People in this story.** [Russell Hooper](#ah-r0304). A useful lullaby depends on choosing the right machine and the right load.

**Personality.** Irene is kind in very practical ways and prickly about being thanked in public. Irene loves being recognized by the people who notice what happens after the guests have left.

**Appearance.** Irene has a flat bridge to the nose and a mobile brow. Look for a cropped cut that shows the shape of the ears. Away from the heat of the kitchen, the familiar outfit is a striped cotton shirt with a train token tied to a zipper pull. In conversation Irene listens with the head tilted a little to the left.

**Under pressure**

- The washer bearing goes quiet mid-song. Irene listens for the missing note and lets maintenance know what changed.
- An unfamiliar person asks for directions but cannot read the street signs. Irene finds out what the destination actually looks like to the traveler and gives directions in those terms.
- If somebody calls for applause, Irene gives the credit to a specific helper and slips out before the photograph.

## Adrian Trent

AH-R0306 · Age 61 · Merchant seafarer · Home — the neighborhood around Market Flats

**Life in the city.** “I still remember a bunkmate singing the same line three days straight.” On Tuesday, near the market by Market Flats, Adrian often waits until the delivery baskets are stacked before going in.

**In their own words.** “I remember a shipboard work song with the same interval. I came ashore to compare them.”

**What Adrian wants.** To compare the seafarer's work song with the recording.

**Family.** [Owen Brandt](../02/15-a-sail-cut-small.md#ah-r0296) is Adrian’s younger cousin. Adrian asks the question the rest of the family avoided. Owen chooses an ordinary story to tell. They tend to get further with an important subject when it arrives in the middle of talk about dinner or work. Asked about *A sail cut small*, Owen says, “I cut a pattern from the wrong edge and ruined my plan.”

**People in this story.** [Pearl Gross](#ah-r0307). A seafarer's tune becomes something the neighbor can feel through the counter.

**Personality.** Likes beautiful, unnecessary things and gets cross with people who mistake poverty for a lack of taste. Adrian takes longer choosing a cheap birthday card than an expensive coat.

**Appearance.** Adrian has a wide brow and a narrow jaw. Look for silver temples and a dark fringe. Back on the street, the familiar outfit is a charcoal zip cardigan with a cloth patch so neatly matched it only shows in strong light. In conversation Adrian draws small circles on a tabletop while waiting.

**Under pressure**

- A tired neighbor asks the singers to move. Adrian asks when the neighbor sleeps and helps arrange a quieter hour.
- A family supper turns into an argument about old debt. Adrian asks each person to say what they believe is actually owed before anybody proposes a payment.
- When a relative dismisses a little luxury, Adrian asks which ordinary pleasure the relative would give up first.

**In their own words.** “My hearing aids flatten the bass. I held the counter and finally felt what people meant.”

**What Pearl wants.** To feel the bass from a safe seat.

**Family.** [Benedict Perreault](../06/13-the-almost-scholarship.md#ah-r0876) is Pearl’s first cousin once removed. Pearl chooses an ordinary story to tell. Benedict asks the question the rest of the family avoided. They tend to get further with an important subject when it arrives in the middle of talk about dinner or work. Asked about *The almost scholarship*, Benedict says, “I am the child's manager at the market. I'll hold one Saturday a month for as long as she wants it.”

**People in this story.** [Adrian Trent](#ah-r0306). A seafarer's tune becomes something the neighbor can feel through the counter.

**Personality.** Pearl can be wonderfully decisive about an emergency and maddeningly undecided about a gift. This is because a person can say precisely what hurts and rarely says exactly what they want.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a black overshirt with pearl buttons and a narrow brass watch set a little fast. Pearl has a scattering of freckles across the bridge of the nose, with silvery hair gathered at the nape. Pearl draws small circles on a tabletop while waiting during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- A colleague says somebody used the wrong name in public. Pearl offers to help correct the printed name and leaves it to the colleague whether any public explanation is wanted.

## Lucia Starr

AH-R0308 · Age 30 · Choreographer · Home — the western blocks near Ferry Street Rooms

**Life in the city.** “I still remember rehearsing in a room with no mirrors.” On Friday, near the public end of the South Shore boardwalk, Lucia checks the shade on the benches before deciding how long to stay.

**In their own words.** “I wrote my first dance to those machines. I want to rehearse before customers start arriving.”

**What Lucia wants.** To rehearse before opening without delaying work.

**Family.** [Nelson Darzi](../07/14-the-carer-s-afternoon.md#ah-r1034) is Lucia’s first cousin once removed. Lucia checks the date twice. Nelson brings something that can still be enjoyed tomorrow. Arrangements do change in their family. Taking a delay seriously without treating it as an insult took both of them years. Asked about *The carer's afternoon*, Nelson says, “I am the parent needing care. My daughter doesn't know I've wanted an afternoon without her questions too.”

**People in this story.** [Alex Gomez](#ah-r0310). The dancer gives the lost visitor directions to a morning rehearsal.

**Personality.** Lucia can be lavish with time and quite careful with cash. A free afternoon is sometimes the biggest gift Lucia can afford to give, and family knows to take it seriously.

**Appearance.** Lucia has auburn hair cut square at the nape, a heart-shaped face and prominent ears, and a favorite piece of clothing, a striped cotton shirt. It usually goes with a seam let out by a narrow strip of contrasting fabric. When somebody else has the floor, Lucia looks down for a moment before saying something difficult.

**Under pressure**

- A tired neighbor asks the singers to move. Lucia asks when the neighbor sleeps and helps arrange a quieter hour.
- A power cut leaves two neighbors waiting on the stairs. Lucia asks which apartment is expecting someone home and makes sure that household knows about the delay.
- If invited to an expensive outing, Lucia suggests meeting afterward and explains why without asking the others to abandon their plans.

## Alex Gomez

AH-R0310 · Age 29 · Visiting tour cook · Home — the neighborhood above Lighthouse Cove

**Life in the city.** “I still remember burning dumplings the morning I left home.” People around Ferris Deli know Alex by a small Tuesday habit. Alex asks for the end slice and listens to the names the counter workers use for regulars.

**In their own words.** “I sat in the wrong laundromat listening for it. A stranger walked me all the way here.”

**What Alex wants.** To thank the stranger who found the correct shop.

**Family.** [Dana Sanders](../08/08-a-summer-marriage.md#ah-r1126) is Alex’s parent’s cousin. Alex asks before bringing somebody along. Dana can always ask for five minutes alone. It keeps a conversation between two people from unexpectedly turning into a family committee. Asked about *A summer marriage*, Dana says, “I sew uniforms. The couple asked for formal clothes they can go straight back to work in.”

**People in this story.** [Lucia Starr](#ah-r0308). The dancer gives the lost visitor directions to a morning rehearsal.

**Personality.** Alex has an appetite for a good argument about little things and works hard to keep a serious disagreement from being turned into entertainment.

**Appearance.** Alex has a wide brow and a narrow jaw. Look for a high puff tied with plain cord. Away from the heat of the kitchen, the familiar outfit is a charcoal zip cardigan with a stitched row of blue crosses along an old repair. In conversation Alex counts on the side of one finger where hardly anybody notices.

**Under pressure**

- A tired neighbor asks the singers to move. Alex asks when the neighbor sleeps and helps arrange a quieter hour.
- A diner goes silent in the middle of a noisy family celebration. Alex takes a small plate to the quieter end of the table and saves the seat beside it.
- When a private quarrel becomes a public joke, Alex says the plainest truthful thing possible and changes the subject.
