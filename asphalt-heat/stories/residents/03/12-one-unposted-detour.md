## Sam Sloan

AH-R0411 · Age 39 · Delivery route planner · Home — the western residential streets

**Life in the city.** “I still remember missing my stop on my first morning as a planner.” Friday often includes a stop at Studio Boulevard, where Sam waits by the window where the whole crossing can be seen without leaning into the road.

**In their own words.** “I made the map but left out the bakery because it wasn't a registered depot.”

**What Sam wants.** To put the unofficial depot on the delivery route.

**Family.** [Rae Meyer](../02/14-saturday-keys.md#ah-r0290) is Sam’s parent’s cousin. Sam chooses whether to use it. Rae saves a place. The invitation means something because declining it has never ended the friendship between their households. Asked about *Saturday keys*, Rae says, “I want one key back permanently. Opening my own door is the part of Saturday I look forward to.”

**People in this story.** [Elise Cotton](#ah-r0417) (notice printer). They redesign the printed diversion around the users it left out.

**Personality.** Sam can be wonderfully decisive about an emergency and maddeningly undecided about a gift. This is because a person can say precisely what hurts and rarely says exactly what they want.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a striped cotton shirt and a partly rubbed-away embroidered initial. Sam has an aquiline nose and a square chin, with a smooth side part with one rebellious curl. Sam touches a knuckle to the chin when searching for a word during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- A delivery customer refuses the late parcel. Sam checks with the intended recipient before leaving a late parcel.
- A frightened child is waiting outside a shuttered shop. Sam stays where the child can see the shop door and asks the neighboring counter to help find the adult expected there.
- If a neighbor offers a costly present, Sam asks for an afternoon to work out whether accepting it would put someone else in debt.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a soft blue checked shirt and a copper-colored clasp that has been repaired by hand. Jeff has fine brows over wide-set eyes, with silver temples and a dark fringe. Jeff touches a knuckle to the chin when searching for a word during a conversation.

**Under pressure**
