## Iva Salas

AH-R1281 · Age 47 · Estranged daughter and retail buyer · Home — the western residential streets

**Life in the city.** “I still remember learning exactly which gate never latched on the night I left.” On Wednesday, near the window at Night Shift Donuts, Iva knows when the going-home crowd and the coming-to-work crowd will both be there.

**In their own words.** “I thought the house would look smaller. The door still feels exactly as high as the day I left.”

**What Iva wants.** To visit without surrendering the right to leave.

**Family.** [Teal Alder](../02/02-five-missing-lunches.md#ah-r0164) is Iva’s younger cousin. Iva decides what stays off the paper. Teal brings the paper and a pen. They have helped each other make decisions that were far easier to discuss once the figures could be moved around. The decisions themselves still belong to the person asking. Asked about *Five missing lunches*, Teal says, “I offered to deliver them and discovered that two people who worked together for years never knew each other's addresses.”

**People in this story.** [Caroline Diaz](#ah-r1282) (foster mother). The foster mother gives the returning adult permission to love more than one home. Other connections include [Arthur Colón](#ah-r1283) (father), [Greta Hassan](#ah-r1287) (younger half sister), [Terry Perry](#ah-r1289) (partner).

**Personality.** Iva feels a room through sound. A door slammed in high spirits can produce the same instant startle as an angry one, and it takes a little while to hear what actually follows.

**Appearance.** Iva has thick, tightly coiled hair, a softly rounded chin under sharp cheekbones, and a favorite piece of clothing, a gray fisher's sweater. It usually goes with a square of clean cloth for polishing smudges. When somebody else has the floor, Iva looks at people's hands before looking at what they carry.

**Under pressure**

- The estranged daughter asks to leave before the meal. Iva helps the daughter leave without forcing a final conversation at the door.
- A coworker arrives late after a night at the hospital. Iva takes one immediate task off their hands and asks when they want to tell the supervisor.
- If an argument gets loud at home, Iva lowers their own voice and asks for one thought at a time.

## Caroline Diaz

AH-R1282 · Age 70 · Retired foster carer · Home — the western residential streets

**Life in the city.** “I still remember washing sheets for an unexpected child who stayed years.” People around the public end of the South Shore boardwalk know Caroline by a small Friday habit. Caroline checks the shade on the benches before deciding how long to stay.

**In their own words.** “I raised her after she left. I want her to know going back doesn't cancel the years she calls me Mum.”

**What Caroline wants.** To keep the daughter's present home open to her.

**Family.** [Avery Lai](../04/11-the-fitting-mirror.md#ah-r0553) is Caroline’s younger relative by marriage. Caroline can always ask for five minutes alone. Avery asks before bringing somebody along. It keeps a conversation between two people from unexpectedly turning into a family committee. Asked about *The fitting mirror*, Avery says, “I used it to practice asking for my old job back.”

**People in this story.** [Iva Salas](#ah-r1281) (foster daughter). The foster mother gives the returning adult permission to love more than one home.

**Personality.** Caroline treats keeping somebody waiting as a serious discourtesy. This can turn a helpful person into an impatient one when everybody else needs a slower conversation.

**Appearance.** Caroline has a deep crease beside the mouth when smiling. Look for a long silver braid. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is an old plum windbreaker with a braided bracelet with a single wooden bead. In conversation Caroline counts on the side of one finger where hardly anybody notices.

**Under pressure**

- The father offers a letter instead of speaking. Caroline asks whether the daughter wants to take the letter now or leave it closed for another day.
- An argument on the pavement makes a bystander feel unsafe. Caroline puts distance between the frightened person and the argument and asks nearby staff for help.
- If a frightened person needs longer, Caroline changes the plan openly and takes responsibility for the delay.

## Arthur Colón

AH-R1283 · Age 75 · Retired mechanic · Home — Old Town

**Life in the city.** “I still remember rehearsing a welcome in front of a blank television.” On Saturday, near the corner outside Coastal Barber, Arthur usually checks the board before the regulars give a more colorful account of the news.

**In their own words.** “I am the father. I rehearsed an explanation for years and forgot to begin with her name.”

**What Arthur wants.** To start the reunion with the daughter's real name.

**Family.** [Lara Hart](../04/02-the-soft-wharf-chalk.md#ah-r0469) is Arthur’s first cousin once removed. Arthur chooses whether they walk home or sit a while. Lara brings a spare shopping bag. That small arrangement gives both a reason to see each other on weeks when asking for company outright feels difficult. Asked about *The soft wharf chalk*, Lara says, “I left a thumbprint when I checked whether the chalk was loose.”

**People in this story.** [Teresa Arendt](#ah-r1288). The father practices his first words while the carpenter finishes the banister. Also in the story is [Iva Salas](#ah-r1281) (daughter).

**Personality.** Arthur is patient with a person learning and fiercely impatient with somebody pretending to know. A half-finished job left where a beginner can see it is an invitation, as far as Arthur is concerned.

**Appearance.** Arthur has tightly curled gray hair, a narrow face with an expressive upper lip, and a favorite piece of clothing, a sand-colored linen shirt. It usually goes with a thin silver ring worn on a cord. When somebody else has the floor, Arthur steadies a cup with both hands while listening.

**Under pressure**

- The estranged daughter asks to leave before the meal. Arthur helps the daughter leave without forcing a final conversation at the door.
- Smoke appears under a shop door after closing. Arthur alerts the nearest occupied shop, stays outside and writes down when the smoke first appeared.
- When someone invents an answer, Arthur asks them to try it in front of the person who will have to live with it.

## Charles Stone

AH-R1286 · Age 42 · Paid carer · Home — the streets inland from Pier Beach

**Life in the city.** “I still remember seeing an old man's private grief during an ordinary paid visit.” People around the market by Market Flats know Charles by a small Monday habit. Charles often waits until the delivery baskets are stacked before going in.

**In their own words.** “I care for the old man. I have no business telling his daughter how frailty ought to make her feel.”

**What Charles wants.** To protect the old man's care without prescribing forgiveness.

**Family.** [Astrid Olsen](../04/08-the-rescue-hut-mural.md#ah-r0525) is Charles’s older cousin. Charles is free to hang up first. Astrid makes the call. Some of their best calls last less than a minute. A change of tone is sometimes all the news either one needs. Asked about *The rescue hut mural*, Astrid says, “I painted the original under a supervision order.”

**People in this story.** [Valentina Jiang](#ah-r1290). The paid carer and the person holding the letter agree that nobody's frailty requires a forced answer.

**Personality.** Charles is cheerful on a bad morning and unexpectedly melancholy after a success. Finishing a hard thing means losing the daily company of the people who worked on it.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a black overshirt with pearl buttons and a narrow brass watch set a little fast. Charles has a strong profile and a little space between the front teeth, with a smooth side part with one rebellious curl. Charles squares up pieces of paper while thinking during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- The father offers a letter instead of speaking. Charles asks whether the daughter wants to take the letter now or leave it closed for another day.
- A frightened child is waiting outside a shuttered shop. Charles asks the nearest shop worker to stay too, then talks about ordinary things until the child can explain who is late.
- When everyone else wants to celebrate immediately, Charles helps set the table, then takes a short turn outside alone.
