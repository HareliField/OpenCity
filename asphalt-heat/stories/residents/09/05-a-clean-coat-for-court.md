# A clean coat for court

[The price of being known](README.md) · [City directory](../directory.md)

**Old Town.** A resident facing a hearing borrows a coat from a person he once cheated. The lender insists on coming to the courthouse to get it back.

## Gareth Rivera

AH-R1241 · Age 48 · Former confidence trickster and repairer · Home — Midtown

**Life in the city.** “I still remember watching a customer's suspicious face become trusting.” People around the corner outside Coastal Barber know Gareth by a small Wednesday habit. Gareth usually checks the board before the regulars give a more colorful account of the news.

**In their own words.** “I cheated him. Borrowing his coat feels worse than borrowing money because I'm wearing his good opinion.”

**What Gareth wants.** To return the coat and address the debt separately.

**Family.** [Cory Halevi](../01/13-the-borrowed-bass.md#ah-r0121) is Gareth’s first cousin once removed. Gareth is allowed to ask about the bad news afterward. Cory calls about good news first. Neither wants every family conversation to begin with a bill, a diagnosis or an apology. Asked about *The borrowed bass*, Cory says, “I heard that rattle every time I played the low E.”

**People in this story.** [Cassandra Joseph](#ah-r1242) (creditor). Lending the coat does not settle the fraud debt. Both men have to live with that distinction. Also in the story is [Desmond Green](#ah-r1244) (adult child).

**Personality.** Gareth is cheerful on a bad morning and unexpectedly melancholy after a success. Finishing a hard thing means losing the daily company of the people who worked on it.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a boxy denim jacket and a copper-colored clasp that has been repaired by hand. Gareth has a flat bridge to the nose and a mobile brow, with a center part and straight chestnut hair. Gareth gathers both sleeves neatly before settling in during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- A stranger asks why the lender came to court. Gareth lets the lender tell or withhold the coat's history.
- Someone offers a cash reward for naming a rumored culprit. Gareth checks privately on the person being talked about and offers to accompany a real witness who wants to speak.
- When everyone else wants to celebrate immediately, Gareth helps set the table, then takes a short turn outside alone.

## Cassandra Joseph

AH-R1242 · Age 57 · Cheated workshop owner · Home — the western blocks near Ferry Street Rooms

**Life in the city.** “I still remember receiving my business ledger with three pages missing.” Friday often includes a stop at Ferris Deli, where Cassandra asks for the end slice and listens to the names the counter workers use for regulars.

**In their own words.** “I lent the coat because his daughter will see him in it. I am still owed money and want no confusion about that.”

**What Cassandra wants.** To see the daughter meet her father in good clothes.

**Family.** [Teresa Glass](../02/15-a-sail-cut-small.md#ah-r0291) is Cassandra’s stepsibling. Cassandra decides what stays off the paper. Teresa brings the paper and a pen. They have helped each other make decisions that were far easier to discuss once the figures could be moved around. The decisions themselves still belong to the person asking. Asked about *A sail cut small*, Teresa says, “I cut out a grocery bag and found the boat's number across the bottom.”

**People in this story.** [Gareth Rivera](#ah-r1241) (debtor). Lending the coat does not settle the fraud debt. Both men have to live with that distinction.

**Personality.** Cassandra finds names easy and faces hard on a crowded street. People who assume they have been snubbed usually get a warmer greeting once they speak.

**Appearance.** Cassandra has auburn hair cut square at the nape, a deep crease beside the mouth when smiling, and a favorite piece of clothing, a brown corduroy jacket. It usually goes with a knotted piece of sail thread on a key ring. When somebody else has the floor, Cassandra gathers both sleeves neatly before settling in.

**Under pressure**

- The borrower wants to return the coat before the hearing. Cassandra checks whether the borrower has something suitable to wear through the hearing.
- A cyclist goes down in a crowded crossing. Cassandra moves the onlookers' bags out of the way, asks the rider what hurts, and asks one named bystander to call for help.
- When somebody waves from across traffic, Cassandra waits at the corner to hear their voice and give them a proper answer.

## Oscar Salas

**Family.** [Maria Delgado](../07/14-the-carer-s-afternoon.md#ah-r1031) is Oscar’s cousin raised in the same household. Oscar brings something that can still be enjoyed tomorrow. **Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a charcoal zip cardigan and a partly rubbed-away embroidered initial. Oscar has a broad nose and a deep dimple on one cheek, with a center part and straight chestnut hair. Oscar looks at people's hands before looking at what they carry during a conversation.

**Under pressure**
