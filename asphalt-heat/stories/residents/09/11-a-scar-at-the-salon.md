# A scar at the salon

[The price of being known](README.md) · [City directory](../directory.md)

**Verve Nail Studio.** Verve is commissioned to photograph a client's hands for an advert. A scar from her years on a fishing crew becomes the focus of the campaign.

## Duncan Stahl

AH-R1301 · Age 46 · Former fishing worker and hotel cleaner · Home — the western blocks near Ferry Street Rooms

**Life in the city.** “I still remember making a scar disappear into my hand's ordinary lines.” On Friday, near Breakwater Way, Duncan stops on the landward side of the cycle track to watch the first boats turn for home.

**In their own words.** “I agreed to show my nails. Nobody asked whether my accident was part of the product.”

**What Duncan wants.** To approve the uses of the scarred hand separately.

**Family.** [Vivian Acosta](../04/04-station-steps-in-rain.md#ah-r0481) is Duncan’s half-sibling. Duncan is free to hang up first. Vivian makes the call. Some of their best calls last less than a minute. A change of tone is sometimes all the news either one needs. Asked about *Station steps in rain*, Vivian says, “I came to complain about my street and held an umbrella over the officer I was going to name.”

**People in this story.** [Ruth Sutton](#ah-r1302). The model and the photographer agree which parts of the hand's history are for sale. Also in the story is [Casey Hunter](#ah-r1303) (former crewmate).

**Personality.** Duncan has a long memory for unfair prices and a short one for small insults. Duncan can get along perfectly well with an enemy provided the account between them is clear.

**Appearance.** Duncan is easy to recognize by close-cropped dark curls and a deep crease beside the mouth when smiling. Usually dressed in a brown corduroy jacket with a pocket notebook shaped by years of sitting on it, Duncan listens with the head tilted a little to the left when listening.

**Under pressure**

- A campaign manager wants to enlarge the scar. Duncan asks the model whether the original permission covers the scar.
- A witness is embarrassed about remembering only a little. Duncan asks where they stood and what they could hear, leaving the rest open instead of filling it with guesses.
- If a friend borrows money, Duncan agrees on an amount both can bear losing before any cash changes hands.

## Ruth Sutton

AH-R1302 · Age 37 · Advertising photographer · Home — the blocks north of the port

**Life in the city.** “I still remember my first commercial photograph where the subject got no print.” People around the blocks below Foundry Lofts know Ruth by a small Friday habit. Ruth checks the little hand-lettered notices around Harbour Supply.

**In their own words.** “I loved the scar in the picture because my own hands look similar. That doesn't let me sell her story.”

**What Ruth wants.** To get the sitter's actual permission to use the picture.

**Family.** [Basil Allard](../05/04-one-clean-day.md#ah-r0631) is Ruth’s stepsibling. Ruth calls back when there is finally a little privacy. Basil leaves the first voice message. In their family a missed ring can mean a bus, a customer, a sleeping child or a bad hour. Neither treats it as an answer in itself. Asked about *One clean day*, Basil says, “I remember exactly who sat beside me when I repeated a year.”

**People in this story.** [Duncan Stahl](#ah-r1301). The model and the photographer agree which parts of the hand's history are for sale.

**Personality.** Ruth takes a rejection personally for a day and analytically on the second morning. Ruth can be generous with a rival once the first night's sleep has intervened.

**Appearance.** Ruth has dark brown curls cut close behind the ears, a flat bridge to the nose and a mobile brow, and a favorite piece of clothing, a long navy raincoat. It usually goes with a pair of reading glasses in a cloth sleeve. When somebody else has the floor, Ruth steadies a cup with both hands while listening.

**Under pressure**

- The model asks to see the unedited print. Ruth gets the model a private look before anybody else comments on the hand.
- A witness is embarrassed about remembering only a little. Ruth writes down their exact words with room left for uncertainty and gives them back to check.
- If someone else wins the opportunity, Ruth turns up the next morning with the one piece of advice that would have helped either of them.

## Casey Hunter

AH-R1303 · Age 49 · Former fishing partner · Home — the blocks north of the port

**Life in the city.** “I still remember hearing the winch the day my partner lost her grip.” Wednesday often includes a stop at the brick passage off the shop row, where Casey takes the longer way when the smell from the ovens reaches the pavement.

**In their own words.** “I am the fishing partner who was there that day. I want the client to know she can tell as much or little as she likes.”

**What Casey wants.** To let the former partner choose what to tell about the accident.

**Family.** [Lena Roth](../02/03-the-sandwich-board.md#ah-r0177) is Casey’s cousin. Casey asks what the birthday guest would like this year. Lena remembers the birthdays. A present that was perfect at sixteen may be completely wrong at forty. They take the trouble of finding out. Asked about *The sandwich board*, Lena says, “I promised the owner her business would survive.”

**People in this story.** [Riley Evans](#ah-r1307). The original fishing partner meets a second worker who will model an injury on separate terms. Also in the story is [Duncan Stahl](#ah-r1301) (former crewmate).

**Personality.** Casey admires skill and distrusts the idea that skill gives anyone permission to be rude. Casey learns fastest from somebody willing to show how they made a mistake.

**Appearance.** Casey is easy to recognize by thick, tightly coiled hair and a narrow face with an expressive upper lip. Usually dressed in a rust-colored canvas coat with a small enamel bird on the lapel, Casey turns fully toward the person speaking when listening.

**Under pressure**

- A campaign manager wants to enlarge the scar. Casey asks the model whether the original permission covers the scar.
- A power cut leaves two neighbors waiting on the stairs. Casey stays where both neighbors can hear a familiar voice and gets news from the caretaker.
- When a beginner is embarrassed, Casey describes a first attempt of their own in enough detail to make the comparison fair.

## Ariel Morse

AH-R1304 · Age 54 · Campaign investor · Home — the western blocks near Ferry Street Rooms

**Life in the city.** “I still remember a campaign meeting where nobody had dirty fingers.” Thursday often includes a stop at the bank steps, where Ariel takes a few minutes to read the public notices, especially the ones written in a hurry.

**What Ariel wants.** To fund an advert the model recognizes as fair.

**Family.** [Kai Shah](../02/09-a-wall-of-birthdays.md#ah-r0239) is Ariel’s cousin raised in the same household. Ariel calls back when there is finally a little privacy. Kai leaves the first voice message. In their family a missed ring can mean a bus, a customer, a sleeping child or a bad hour. Neither treats it as an answer in itself. Asked about *A wall of birthdays*, Kai says, “I came to negotiate a sale and discovered my own childhood nickname behind the radiator.”

**People in this story.** [Silas Voss](#ah-r1309). The investor cannot approve the campaign until the copywriter replaces its presumptions.

**Personality.** Ariel has a strong competitive streak and a very soft spot for the person finishing last. A prize tastes better when everybody had enough time to enter.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a mustard canvas overshirt and a fabric zip pull cut from a school scarf. Ariel has a long face and high cheekbones, with a high puff tied with plain cord. Ariel presses the lips together to keep a smile in during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- The model asks to see the unedited print. Ariel gets the model a private look before anybody else comments on the hand.
- An envelope of wages is found under a café chair. Ariel finds a second person to witness its return so an honest mistake won't cost a worker their pay.
- If accused of favoritism, Ariel explains exactly what help was offered and offers the same practical help to the accuser.

## Louise Hooper

AH-R1305 · Age 25 · Nail studio assistant · Home — the western blocks near Ferry Street Rooms

**Life in the city.** “I still remember recognizing my own work on a poster that named someone else.” On Friday, near the public road outside the Port of Asphalt, Louise can recognize shift change by the lull between two groups of walkers.

**In their own words.** “I am the junior worker who polished the scarred hand. My name was left off the credit sheet.”

**What Louise wants.** To get the nail technician's credit restored.

**Family.** [Kit Gill](../06/15-winter-afloat.md#ah-r0895) is Louise’s older cousin. Louise can always ask for five minutes alone. Kit asks before bringing somebody along. It keeps a conversation between two people from unexpectedly turning into a family committee. Asked about *Winter afloat*, Kit says, “I loaned him the key to the workshop shower.”

**People in this story.** [Eva Bhandari](#ah-r1308). The studio assistant and the wall owner discuss giving local workers their own credits.

**Personality.** Louise reads little signals of discomfort very accurately and has an unfortunate habit of overruling people for their own good. The friends who say stop are the ones Louise trusts most.

**Appearance.** Louise has a strong profile and a little space between the front teeth. Look for a cropped cut that shows the shape of the ears. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a striped cotton shirt with a partly rubbed-away embroidered initial. In conversation Louise squares up pieces of paper while thinking.

**Under pressure**

- A campaign manager wants to enlarge the scar. Louise asks the model whether the original permission covers the scar.
- A stranger waves a phone camera in a crying person's face. Louise asks the person crying whether they want to go somewhere quieter, leaving them control over the next step.
- If told to back off, Louise does so immediately and stays available at a distance the other person chooses.

**Appearance.** Bailey is easy to recognize by a swept fringe and a closely clipped nape and two dimples that appear only with a full smile. Usually dressed in a striped cotton shirt with a tiny red thread tied through the lowest buttonhole, Bailey grins on one side while the eyes stay serious when listening.

**Under pressure**

- If the joke lands badly, Bailey seeks out the person it hurt before telling anybody why it was supposed to be funny.

## Riley Evans

AH-R1307 · Age 45 · Factory worker · Home — Old Town

**Life in the city.** “I still remember working my first shift after the bandages came off.” Thursday often includes a stop at Lighthouse Cove, where Riley takes a few minutes by the low rocks, where conversation sounds different as the tide turns.

**In their own words.** “I used to hide my fingers after a factory injury. I asked to sit as a separate model on my own terms.”

**What Riley wants.** To make a separate portrait on independent terms.

**Family.** [Ignacio Schmidt](../08/15-the-skipper-goes-ashore.md#ah-r1191) is Riley’s second cousin. Riley brings something that can still be enjoyed tomorrow. Ignacio checks the date twice. Arrangements do change in their family. Taking a delay seriously without treating it as an insult took both of them years. Asked about *The skipper goes ashore*, Ignacio says, “I can face a hard crossing and can't face my crew seeing me wait for the nausea to settle.”

**People in this story.** [Casey Hunter](#ah-r1303). The original fishing partner meets a second worker who will model an injury on separate terms.

**Personality.** Riley listens for the familiar version of a story and then for the one detail that changes it. Friends bring problems here when they want to be heard twice.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a dark green knitted vest and a tiny red thread tied through the lowest buttonhole. Riley has a wide brow and a narrow jaw, with short black hair swept back. Riley starts to lift a hand, then waits to be invited to speak during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- A campaign manager wants to enlarge the scar. Riley asks the model whether the original permission covers the scar.
- A colleague says somebody used the wrong name in public. Riley repeats the correct name quietly, making the correction easy to hear without turning it into a performance.
- When a second witness disagrees, Riley keeps the accounts separate until the witnesses have had a chance to meet.

## Keiko Cross

AH-R1310 · Age 51 · Postcard seller · Home — Coast Ridge

**Life in the city.** “I still remember writing a nearly anonymous postcard from a hospital café.” On Thursday, near Studio Boulevard, Keiko waits by the window where the whole crossing can be seen without leaning into the road.

**In their own words.** “I bought a plain postcard of the hand. I want the client to choose what to write on the back.”

**What Keiko wants.** To let the model decide what the postcard says.

**Family.** [Orla Jain](../03/07-overdue-by-a-lifetime.md#ah-r0368) is Keiko’s younger cousin. Keiko decides what stays off the paper. Orla brings the paper and a pen. They have helped each other make decisions that were far easier to discuss once the figures could be moved around. The decisions themselves still belong to the person asking. Asked about *Overdue by a lifetime*, Orla says, “I bought the whole box for the cookbook on top.”

**People in this story.** [Bailey Solis](#ah-r1306). The unretouched picture and the blank postcard leave the client two personal choices.

**Personality.** Keiko never tires of being shown how somebody makes a living. Keiko can ask an expert question of a craftsperson and still mishandle a simple emotional cue from a friend.

**Appearance.** Keiko has a heart-shaped face and prominent ears. Look for short black hair swept back. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a moss-green cardigan with a train token tied to a zipper pull. In conversation Keiko steadies a cup with both hands while listening.

**Under pressure**

- The model asks to see the unedited print. Keiko gets the model a private look before anybody else comments on the hand.
- A customer is short at the till while a queue forms. Keiko keeps the customer's place while they check the amount and gets the impatient people talking about something else.
- When a relative hints at needing company, Keiko may initially offer a repair, but sits down as soon as the request becomes clear.
