# A call heard twice

## Imani Ford

AH-R1231 · Age 45 · Patrol officer · Home — Coast Ridge · Department ID ah-pd-133 · Patrol, watch 3, badge 133

**Life in the city.** “I still remember a disconnected call followed by a whole afternoon wondering.” On Thursday, near the approach to Seabright Court, Imani likes to watch the beach traffic thin enough for workers going the other way.

**In their own words.** “I took the first call and lost the connection. I have never known whether she got somebody back.”

**What Imani wants.** To learn how the interrupted call ended.

**Family.** [Wesley Joseph](../02/02-five-missing-lunches.md#ah-r0161) is Imani’s half-sibling. Imani is free to hang up first. Wesley makes the call. Some of their best calls last less than a minute. A change of tone is sometimes all the news either one needs. Asked about *Five missing lunches*, Wesley says, “We used to swap the sweet things. With my new shift I don't know anybody's favorite.”

**People in this story.** [Fern Lin](#ah-r1232) (second call taker). Restoring both voices to the letter is their first shared task after the interrupted call.

**Personality.** Imani listens for the familiar version of a story and then for the one detail that changes it. Friends bring problems here when they want to be heard twice.

**Appearance.** Imani is easy to recognize by close-cropped dark curls and a small notch in the left eyebrow. Usually dressed in a plum-colored cardigan with a comb in a sleeve sewn into the lining, Imani gathers both sleeves neatly before settling in when listening.

**Under pressure**

- A supervisor offers thanks to only one call taker. Imani gets both halves of the call into the account before a name goes up.
- An unfamiliar person asks for directions but cannot read the street signs. Imani walks with them as far as the first landmark and points to the next one from there.
- When a second witness disagrees, Imani keeps the accounts separate until the witnesses have had a chance to meet.

## Fern Lin

AH-R1232 · Age 42 · Police communications dispatcher · Home — Coast Ridge · Department ID ah-pd-145 · Communications, watch 3, badge 145

**Life in the city.** “I still remember a colleague giving me exactly one fact before handing over a voice.” Thursday often includes a stop at the bank steps, where Fern takes a few minutes to read the public notices, especially the ones written in a hurry.

**In their own words.** “I took the second call and inherited the trust the first voice had built. I want the writer to hear both names.”

**What Fern wants.** To credit both conversations equally.

**Family.** [Stella Choi](../10/09-a-house-divided-kindly.md#ah-r1436) is Fern’s cousin. Fern offers the first invitation. Stella gets to change the plan. They learned that a promise to visit is easier to keep when changing a time does not count as letting down the whole family. Asked about *A house divided kindly*, Stella says, “I used to squeeze through the gap to see my best friend.”

**People in this story.** [Imani Ford](#ah-r1231) (first call taker). Restoring both voices to the letter is their first shared task after the interrupted call.

**Personality.** Fern talks very little about private fear and recognizes it quickly in strangers. Offering a choice of seats is one of Fern's ways of saying that it is all right to leave.

**Appearance.** Fern is easy to recognize by a soft fringe that falls across one eyebrow and a deep crease beside the mouth when smiling. Usually dressed in a pale blue work shirt with a pocket notebook shaped by years of sitting on it, Fern rubs a thumb along the edge of the nearest pocket when listening.

**Under pressure**

- The resident asks for a reply in somebody's own words. Fern asks the caller how they want to receive a personal reply.
- A diner goes silent in the middle of a noisy family celebration. Fern asks one close relative to check in privately instead of stopping the party for a public inquiry.
- When pressed to explain someone else's silence, Fern says only what happened and lets the absent person keep their reasons.

**Appearance.** Ravi has an aquiline nose and a square chin. Look for a swept fringe and a closely clipped nape. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a cropped bottle-green coat with a stitched row of blue crosses along an old repair. **Under pressure**
