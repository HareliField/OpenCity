## Malcolm Garrett

AH-R1311 · Age 46 · Driving instructor · Home — the neighborhood above Lighthouse Cove

**Life in the city.** “I still remember cleaning a steering wheel I still couldn't afford to learn on.” On Saturday, near the window at Night Shift Donuts, Malcolm knows when the going-home crowd and the coming-to-work crowd will both be there.

**In their own words.** “I paid for my license by cleaning the instructor's flat. I don't want to send another learner to scrub a floor.”

**What Malcolm wants.** To provide a funded lesson without unpaid labor in exchange.

**Family.** [Maria Dias](../08/09-the-third-bedroom.md#ah-r1138) is Malcolm’s second cousin. Malcolm gets to change the plan. Maria offers the first invitation. They learned that a promise to visit is easier to keep when changing a time does not count as letting down the whole family. Asked about *The third bedroom*, Maria says, “I can rent a room to the lodger but I have a noisy night shift.”

**People in this story.** [Parker Gao](#ah-r1312). A free lesson starts with hearing an honest question about eyesight. Also in the story is [Gareth Mercer](#ah-r1316) (funded learner).

**Personality.** Malcolm uses precise names for tools and very approximate names for feelings. People who know Malcolm well listen to what gets repaired after a difficult conversation.

**Appearance.** On an ordinary day, look for a soft blue checked shirt and a braided bracelet with a single wooden bead. Malcolm has a small notch in the left eyebrow, with a loose dark braid. Malcolm counts on the side of one finger where hardly anybody notices during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- Two people qualify for the same funded lesson. Malcolm asks both applicants which transport difficulty one lesson would actually address.
- The last bus leaves while an elderly stranger is still crossing toward it. Malcolm asks the stranger who will be expecting them, then helps find a way to tell that person.
- After a family quarrel, Malcolm brings back the borrowed thing in good condition and tries the conversation again without an audience.
