# What the street remembers

[All residents](../README.md) · [Alphabetical directory](../directory.md)

Residents 451–600. Each episode follows ten people who are affected differently by the same event.

| Episode | People | Opening situation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The button card](01-the-button-card.md) | 451–460 | A card of mismatched buttons beside the Wash House till is labeled TAKE THE ONE YOU LOST. |
| [The soft wharf chalk](02-the-soft-wharf-chalk.md) | 461–470 | An old chalk message survives on the underside of a port loading platform. |
| [The savings club](03-the-savings-club.md) | 471–480 | A savings club that meets on the bank steps has enough money for exactly one member to take a long-delayed trip. |
| [Station steps in rain](04-station-steps-in-rain.md) | 481–490 | The Old Town station invites neighbors to sit in on an open afternoon. Heavy rain sends everyone to the same shallow portico. |
| [The dead man's tab](05-the-dead-man-s-tab.md) | 491–500 | A Ferris Deli tab survives the death of a regular who did time for fraud. Three people offer to settle it. |
| [The waiting-room plant](06-the-waiting-room-plant.md) | 501–510 | A leaning hospital plant outlives three wards and acquires a hand-lettered visiting schedule. |
| [A borrowed name](07-a-borrowed-name.md) | 511–520 | A local writer at Paper & Pine discovers that a minor character shares a name and a childhood incident with a regular customer. |
| [The rescue hut mural](08-the-rescue-hut-mural.md) | 521–530 | A faded figure on the Pier Beach rescue hut looks different depending on where one stands. |
| [The porch auction](09-the-porch-auction.md) | 531–540 | A household sells its porch chairs before moving into a smaller flat. Each chair brings a different neighbor to the gate. |

| [The fitting mirror](11-the-fitting-mirror.md) | 551–560 | Verve and Juniper share a standing mirror while one shop is being replastered. |
| [The ladder crossing](12-the-ladder-crossing.md) | 561–570 | A long ladder carried by hand over a Coast Ridge crossing brings two streams of people to an accidental halt. |
| [A play in a pocket](13-a-play-in-a-pocket.md) | 571–580 | A youth group at the downtown fountain performs short plays for one audience member at a time. |
| [Laundry for a stranger](14-laundry-for-a-stranger.md) | 581–590 | A western meal volunteer finds a carefully ironed stranger's shirt in with an elderly client's kitchen linen. |
| [The engine lullaby](15-the-engine-lullaby.md) | 591–600 | A moored boat at South Shore Harbor makes a low humming sound that a neighbor remembers from childhood. |
