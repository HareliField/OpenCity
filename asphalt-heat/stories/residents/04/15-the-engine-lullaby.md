## Alice Ortiz

AH-R0591 · Age 42 · Boat owner's grown child and teacher · Home — Coast Ridge

**Life in the city.** “I still remember a childhood nap over the sound of a bilge pump.” Tuesday often includes a stop at the market by Market Flats, where Alice often waits until the delivery baskets are stacked before going in.

**In their own words.** “My father took me aboard whenever I couldn't sleep. I want the same sound for the night he moves into care.”

**What Alice wants.** To get the father a recording for the move into care.

**Family.** [Carol Mack](../08/11-the-hair-after-illness.md#ah-r1157) is Alice’s second cousin. Alice remembers the birthdays. Carol asks what the birthday guest would like this year. A present that was perfect at sixteen may be completely wrong at forty. They take the trouble of finding out. Asked about *The hair after illness*, Carol says, “I met her during treatment and have never seen the woman her friends keep insisting she used to be.”

**People in this story.** [Cecil Aubin](#ah-r0599). The engine once rocked a child and now keeps two adults company beside a silent hull.

**Personality.** Alice talks very little about private fear and recognizes it quickly in strangers. Offering a choice of seats is one of Alice's ways of saying that it is all right to leave.

**Appearance.** Back on the street, look for a sand-colored linen shirt and a little coil of soft measuring tape. Alice has a deep crease beside the mouth when smiling, with short, uneven waves that refuse to lie flat. Alice looks down for a moment before saying something difficult during a conversation.

**Under pressure**

- The dock engine falls silent in mid-recording. Alice notes the time the hum stopped before discussing a replacement recording.
- A cyclist goes down in a crowded crossing. Alice moves the onlookers' bags out of the way, asks the rider what hurts, and asks one named bystander to call for help.
- When pressed to explain someone else's silence, Alice says only what happened and lets the absent person keep their reasons.

## Ren Berry

AH-R0592 · Age 64 · Engine tuner · Home — the streets around Foundry Lofts

**Life in the city.** “I still remember tuning an engine in the dark to spare a sleeping crew.” On Monday, near the window at Night Shift Donuts, Ren knows when the going-home crowd and the coming-to-work crowd will both be there.

**In their own words.** “I tuned that engine solely by ear. I can hear which part was replaced while I was away.”

**What Ren wants.** To identify the change in the engine sound.

**Family.** [Allen Mora](../08/09-the-third-bedroom.md#ah-r1135) is Ren’s younger cousin. Ren offers the first invitation. Allen gets to change the plan. They learned that a promise to visit is easier to keep when changing a time does not count as letting down the whole family. Asked about *The third bedroom*, Allen says, “I do the assessment. I brought a tape measure and ended up drawing two alternate plans on the kitchen floor.”

**People in this story.** [Sky Avery](#ah-r0595) (parts supplier). A mechanic demonstrates a familiar note to the parts supplier's apprentice.

**Personality.** Ren prefers exact instructions at work and pleasant surprises at home. The two standards occasionally collide, especially when a relative thinks dropping by counts as notice.

**Appearance.** Ren has a neat white crop, a long face and high cheekbones, and a favorite piece of clothing, a brown corduroy jacket. It usually goes with a square of clean cloth for polishing smudges. When somebody else has the floor, Ren grins on one side while the eyes stay serious.

**Under pressure**

- A family offers to pay to keep the old sound. Ren asks the engine owner what can be kept without postponing necessary repairs.
- A traveler asks for the last seat at the bus stop. Ren looks for a second safe place to sit and stays long enough for the waiting people to settle.
- When a visitor arrives unexpectedly, Ren clears one chair and says honestly how long the visit can last.

**Appearance.** Jacqueline has a heart-shaped face and prominent ears. Look for auburn hair cut square at the nape. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a rust-colored canvas coat with a pocket notebook shaped by years of sitting on it. In conversation Jacqueline raises one eyebrow long before saying yes.

**Under pressure**

- The dock engine falls silent in mid-recording. Jacqueline notes the time the hum stopped before discussing a replacement recording.

- When a relative hints at needing company, Jacqueline may initially offer a repair, but sits down as soon as the request becomes clear.

## Alex Carbone

**Family.** [Oakley Ibrahim](../03/06-the-hospital-radio.md#ah-r0360) is Alex’s older relative by marriage. Alex gets to change the plan. Oakley offers the first invitation. **People in this story.** [Amanda Kerr](#ah-r0594). The singer and tune arranger each try setting a melody against the harbor's actual note.

**Personality.** Alex is patient with a person learning and fiercely impatient with somebody pretending to know. A half-finished job left where a beginner can see it is an invitation, as far as Alex is concerned.

**Appearance.** Alex has a small notch in the left eyebrow. Look for dark brown curls cut close behind the ears. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a mustard canvas overshirt with a pen clipped inside the neckline. In conversation Alex draws small circles on a tabletop while waiting.

**Under pressure**

- A family offers to pay to keep the old sound. Alex asks the engine owner what can be kept without postponing necessary repairs.
- A customer is short at the till while a queue forms. Alex asks the cashier quietly which item can be set aside without making the customer explain their finances to the line.
- When someone invents an answer, Alex asks them to try it in front of the person who will have to live with it.
