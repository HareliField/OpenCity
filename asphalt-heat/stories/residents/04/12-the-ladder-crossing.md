## Arlo Lee

AH-R0561 · Age 51 · Sign painter · Home — the streets inland from Pier Beach

**Life in the city.** “I still remember holding the far end of my father's ladder at dusk.” Friday often includes a stop at Lighthouse Cove, where Arlo takes a few minutes by the low rocks, where conversation sounds different as the tide turns.

**In their own words.** “I was at the front and couldn't see the painter crying at the back. It was his late father's ladder.”

**What Arlo wants.** To ask the painter about the father behind the cedar ladder.

**Family.** [Cynthia Moran](../02/10-the-bitter-orange.md#ah-r0248) is Arlo’s younger cousin. A family member returned from a difficult year elsewhere and wanted dinner without a welcome speech. They made such a point of ordinary dinner that it became a permanent tradition. Arlo offers a person something useful to do as soon as they come in. Asked about *The bitter orange*, Cynthia says, “She had my name wrong for twenty years. I put the wrong name on my contribution to the recipe.”

**People in this story.** [Javier Lam](#ah-r0565). The person carrying the ladder explains why a modern replacement is an awkward gift.

**Personality.** Arlo prefers exact instructions at work and pleasant surprises at home. The two standards occasionally collide, especially when a relative thinks dropping by counts as notice.

**Appearance.** Arlo has a strong profile and a little space between the front teeth. Look for a high puff tied with plain cord. On an ordinary day, the familiar outfit is a cream pullover with a replacement button a shade darker than the others. In conversation Arlo holds eye contact until the other person has finished.

**Under pressure**

- A driver sounds a horn halfway through the crossing. Arlo asks for a pause until the people carrying the ladder can clear the crossing safely.
- Somebody says a missing friend must be avoiding their calls. Arlo asks what the friend actually said last time and checks whether anybody is expecting them at home.
- When a visitor arrives unexpectedly, Arlo clears one chair and says honestly how long the visit can last.

- When a relative hints at needing company, Valentina may initially offer a repair, but sits down as soon as the request becomes clear.

**Family.** [Ashley Hsu](../10/08-no-lifeguard-at-dusk.md#ah-r1421) is Javier’s half-sibling. Javier makes room at the table. Ashley brings whatever was actually growing or affordable that week. The meal sometimes produces a solution. Even when it does not, everyone goes away knowing what the other household is up against. Asked about *No lifeguard at dusk*, Ashley says, “I have watched this woman swim every evening.”

**People in this story.** [Arlo Lee](#ah-r0561). The person carrying the ladder explains why a modern replacement is an awkward gift. Also in the story is [Dario Moreau](#ah-r0564) (crossing surveyor).

**Appearance.** Javier is easy to recognize by a dark undercut with a longer wave on top and a crooked front tooth visible when laughing. Usually dressed in a moss-green cardigan with a fabric zip pull cut from a school scarf, Javier rubs a thumb along the edge of the nearest pocket when listening.

**Under pressure**
