## Karina Hamilton

AH-R0501 · Age 48 · Plant nursery worker · Home — Old Town

**Life in the city.** “I still remember carrying my first rooted cutting in a paper straw.” People around the public end of the South Shore boardwalk know Karina by a small Wednesday habit. Karina checks the shade on the benches before deciding how long to stay.

**In their own words.** “I took the original cutting on the day my husband came home. I want to root another for his brother.”

**What Karina wants.** To root a cutting for the husband's brother.

**Family.** [Lina Farrell](../06/09-a-room-for-a-father.md#ah-r0833) is Karina’s half-sibling. Karina picks the meeting place. Lina sets the time to leave. It lets them stay close without turning a family visit into the loss of an entire day. They have both had stretches when that distinction mattered. Asked about *A room for a father*, Lina says, “I am the second wife's cousin. I can tell these people about a good father they never got.”

**People in this story.** [Elsa Guzman](#ah-r0505). A rooted cutting is small enough to visit a patient who lacks ordinary callers.

**Personality.** Karina has an appetite for a good argument about little things and works hard to keep a serious disagreement from being turned into entertainment.

**Appearance.** Karina has dark brown curls cut close behind the ears, a small notch in the left eyebrow, and a favorite piece of clothing, a charcoal zip cardigan. It usually goes with a pen clipped inside the neckline. When somebody else has the floor, Karina squares up pieces of paper while thinking.

**Under pressure**

- A ward wants the plant for the full week. Karina checks the visiting list for the patients who will miss their turn.
- A family supper turns into an argument about old debt. Karina asks each person to say what they believe is actually owed before anybody proposes a payment.
- When a private quarrel becomes a public joke, Karina says the plainest truthful thing possible and changes the subject.

## Anton Shah

AH-R0507 · Age 48 · Hospital schoolteacher · Home — Eastside

**Life in the city.** “I still remember a child's shadow puppet shaped like a leaf.” Thursday often includes a stop at the foot of Harbor Viaduct, where Anton pauses where the machinery overhead makes two people stand close enough to hear each other.

**What Anton wants.** To give the isolated pupil a new leaf shadow.

**Family.** [Pat Fraser](../05/11-the-free-fringe.md#ah-r0707) is Anton’s cousin. Anton calls about good news first. Pat is allowed to ask about the bad news afterward. Neither wants every family conversation to begin with a bill, a diagnosis or an apology. Asked about *The free fringe*, Pat says, “I heard their conversation through the wall and realized I owed my old shop partner a call.”

**People in this story.** [Jay Knight](#ah-r0503). A patient remembered by the smell of leaves becomes part of the child's shadow lesson.

**Appearance.** Anton is easy to recognize by short, uneven waves that refuse to lie flat and a heart-shaped face and prominent ears. Usually dressed in a boxy denim jacket with a printed handkerchief folded into the pocket, Anton starts to lift a hand, then waits to be invited to speak when listening.

**Under pressure**
