Old Town homicide

The finding

Toby Lin saw Opal Finch shoot Hollis Fry and recognized Finch from the Old Town counter. Officer Ben Ortiz logged three exhibits. One was a sample from Fry. The recovered holdall carried both Fry’s and Finch’s prints. Analyst Ada Flynn matched Finch’s print, corroborating Lin’s statement. Detective Kai Chen submitted the finding, and Commander Maya Scott authorized the bulletin.
