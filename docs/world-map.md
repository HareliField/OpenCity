# Commonstreet world map

The playable city uses a 16 × 16 grid of 64-metre blocks. The main grid spans 1,024 metres on each side. The shore extends east and the freight terminal extends south. In code and agent mode, +x is east, +z is south, and y is height. In game, open the round radar for a north-up map. The map and agent command `{"op":"places"}` provide the current destinations.

| Area | Find it in play | Connected systems |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting shop row and Old Town | Start by Verve Nail Studio. Follow the row to Paper & Pine Books, Night Shift Donuts, and the Wash House. Visit Old Town headquarters from the map or use the `?visit=police` start. | Shop entrances, residents and nearby crossings, the opening on-foot route, and police cases and watches. |
| Downtown and Market Street | Cross the park toward the bank or visit Tideline Market and Market Flats. The flats have a cellar path to Tidewater Pump House. | Shared lift, shop counters, roof access, feeders, pressure, drainage, and sanitary return. |
| Western residential streets | Visit the Ferry Street garden blocks between Maple Street and Ridge Road. Look for furnished cottages, gardens, and service leads. | Residential access, pedestrian clearances, water and sanitary services. |
| Palm Street and Marina Street | Start at the 24-Hour Diner or Marina Coffee, then follow Marina east toward Seabreeze Market and Coastal Vinyl. | Shops, independently accessible lifts and stairs, upper flats, and coastal street traffic. |
| Breakwater Way and Pier Beach | Find the parking-protected cycle track and follow the boardwalk to the steps down to Pier Beach. | Street and pedestrian paths, shelter, beach weather, and changes in the grade and surface. |
| Lighthouse Cove and South Shore Harbor | Follow the shore south to the keeper's house and lighthouse. Continue to the marina with the anchor marker. | Walkable lighthouse stairs, optic and feeder, surf, docks, and shoreline access. |
| Warehouses and Port of Asphalt | Harbor Supply faces Dock Street. Take either port gate south of the warehouse district for yards, rail sidings and the quay. | Delivery clearance, moving cranes, cargo and shutters, service vehicles, and water edges. |
| Harbor Viaduct and Coast Ridge Road | Follow the ramp signs in the southern half of the city. Coast Ridge Road crosses above an eastern ramp of the viaduct. | Layered road surfaces, support and sign clearance, turning routes, and travel at different elevations. |

## Record a place consistently

When changing a place, use its current in-game name, street, building entrance or floor, and nearest map landmark. Add coordinates and facing if a contributor needs them to reproduce the scene. Include y at an overpass, lift, or other stacked surface. Verify the affected walking, driving, delivery or service routes.

For people use the [resident directory](../commonstreet/stories/residents/directory.md) and existing resident IDs. Police staff also have department IDs. An existing business or room should retain relationships and access that other contributions rely on. If an entry in this map changes, update it with the relevant code or canon change.
